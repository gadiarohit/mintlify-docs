[**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home) lets you break a regular schedule day into multiple slots, so you can easily take time out of the calendar for consistent events like lunch breaks, coffee breaks, or recreational breaks. You can open specific slots for specific days, copy those slots to other days, or open completely different slots on different days. It's your Cal ID, your choice.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a schedule">
    Go to **Availability** and open the schedule you want to adjust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add another slot to a day">
    Click the **"\+" icon** next to a day to add a second time slot, leaving a gap between slots for the break you want to protect.

    <Frame>
      ![Multiple time slots configured for a day](/images/availability/multiple-time-slots-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reuse or vary slots across days">
    Copy the slots to other days to keep your week consistent, or open different slots on different days.
  </Step>
</Steps>
