Go through these steps if you already have a **WhatsApp Business App** installed on your mobile device and want to connect it.

<Note>
  Your address and Tax ID must be verified to trigger the message.
</Note>

## Select connection method

1. **Click on "Connect WhatsApp Business app"** - Choose this option from the integration screen
2. This opens the connection process for your existing app

<Frame>
  ![Connect WhatsApp Business app option](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-1.png)
</Frame>

## Enter your phone number

1. **Enter Your WhatsApp Business Phone Number** - Type the phone number associated with your WhatsApp Business app
2. Only **verified phone numbers** will be shown here.
3. Click **Next** to proceed

<Frame>
  ![Entering the WhatsApp Business phone number](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-2.png)
</Frame>

## Get your access code

1. **Click on "Use an access code instead"** - Select this option to get a secure access code and note down the access code provided.

<Frame>
  ![Using an access code instead](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-3.png)
</Frame>

## Enter code on your mobile device

1. **Open WhatsApp Business App** on your mobile phone
2. **Go to Menu or Settings** - Tap on the menu icon
3. **Select "Account"** - Navigate to your account settings
4. **Select "Business platform"** - Choose the business platform option
5. **Enter the Access Code** - Paste the code you copied earlier
6. **Click on "Next"** - Proceed with the connection
7. **Paste the Generated Code**

<Frame>
  ![Entering the access code in the WhatsApp Business app](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-4.png)
</Frame>

## Enter additional data

1. **Fill in Required Information** - Complete any additional details requested
2. **Click "Next"** - Move forward in the process

<Frame>
  ![Filling in additional required information](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-5.png)
</Frame>

## Confirm connection

1. **Review All Details** - Check that everything is correct
2. **Click "Confirm"** - Confirm the connection setup

<Frame>
  ![Confirming the connection setup](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-6.png)
</Frame>

## Scan QR code

1. **A QR Code Page Will Appear** - You will see a QR code on your screen
2. **Scan the Code** - Use your mobile device to scan the QR code
3. **Follow the On-Screen Instructions** - Complete any additional steps shown

<Frame>
  ![Scanning the QR code to complete the connection](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-7.png)
</Frame>
