Add clickable icons for your professional social media accounts directly to your public booking page. It is a great way to build trust and let clients explore your work before they book a meeting.

## Supported platforms

You can link the following platforms to your profile:

- **LinkedIn**
- **Facebook**
- **Twitter**
- **Instagram**
- **YouTube**
- **GitHub**

## Step-by-step configuration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Sign in to your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Custom Branding">
    Select the **Custom Branding** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate Social profiles">
    Scroll down to the **Social profiles** section.

    <Frame>
      ![Social profiles section](/images/branding/social-profiles-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the feature">
    Ensure the toggle at the top-right of this section is switched **ON**.

    <Warning>
      If the toggle is off, your social icons will not appear on your booking page even if you add links.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your links">
    Copy the full URL of your social profile (e.g., `https://linkedin.com/in/yourname`) and paste it into the corresponding field for each platform.

    <Frame>
      ![Add social profile links](/images/branding/social-profiles-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update and save">
    Click the blue **Update** button at the bottom of the Social profiles box to save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Visible changes on your page

Once enabled and updated, small, professional icons for each platform will appear on your public booking page.

- **Automatic icons**: You don't need to upload icons; Cal ID automatically generates the correct logo for each link you provide.
- **Clickable links**: When a guest clicks an icon, your social profile opens in a new tab, keeping your booking page open in the background.

<Frame>
  ![Social icons on public booking page](/images/branding/social-profiles-3.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  Only link the profiles that are relevant to your professional brand. If your Instagram is personal, it's best to leave that field blank and only link professional platforms like LinkedIn or GitHub.
</Tip>
