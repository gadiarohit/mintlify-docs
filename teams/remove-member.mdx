This document details the steps to remove a member from an existing team in Cal ID.

## Steps to Remove a Member

1. **Log in to Cal ID**: Access your account dashboard.
2. **Go to Teams**: Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
3. **Access Team Menu**: Locate the team, click the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Edit team**.

<Frame>
  ![Edit team option](/images/teams/remove-member-1.png)
</Frame>

4. **Go to Members Tab**: Click on the **Members** tab at the top of the page.
5. **Initiate Removal**: Locate the member you wish to remove, click the **three horizontal dots** next to their name, and select **Remove**.

<Frame>
  ![Remove option next to a member](/images/teams/remove-member-2.png)
</Frame>

6. **Confirm Action**: Follow any on-screen prompts to confirm the removal.

<Frame>
  ![Confirming member removal](/images/teams/remove-member-3.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  Only **Owners** or **Admins** have the permission to remove members from a team.
</Note>
