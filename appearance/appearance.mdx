In **Cal ID**, the **Appearance** settings allow you to personalize the visual experience of your account. You can customize both your internal dashboard theme and the layout your guests see on your public booking page.

## Complete setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Appearance tab">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your Appearance">
    Follow the detailed points below to set your themes and layouts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the final **Update** button at the very bottom.
  </Step>

## Setting your dashboard theme

This section controls the appearance of your logged-in dashboard. It is for your eyes only and does not affect your customers.

- **System Default**: Automatically matches your computer or browser's light/dark mode settings.
- **Light**: Sets a clean, bright interface with a white background.

<Frame>
  ![Light dashboard theme](/images/appearance/appearance-1.png)
</Frame>

- **Dark**: Sets a high-contrast dark interface, which can be easier on the eyes in low-light environments.

<Frame>
  ![Dark dashboard theme](/images/appearance/appearance-2.png)
</Frame>

- **How to apply**: Select your preferred theme and click the **Update** button directly below that section.

## Setting your public booking page theme

This section determines the theme your guests see when they visit your link to book a meeting.

- **Customization**: Just like the dashboard, you can choose between **System default**, **Light**, or **Dark**.

<Frame>
  ![Booking page theme options](/images/appearance/appearance-3.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Booking page theme preview](/images/appearance/appearance-4.png)
</Frame>

- **Pro Tip**: Choose a theme that matches your brand's professional aesthetic. Many users prefer "Light" for a traditional business feel or "Dark" for a modern, tech-focused look.
- **How to apply**: Select the theme and click the **Update** button in the Booking page theme section.

<Frame>
  ![Apply booking page theme](/images/appearance/appearance-5.png)

## Choosing and customizing your booking layout

The **Booking layout** determines how your available time slots are presented to the world. You have the power to decide which views are available and which one greets the user first.

### The three layout options

**Month View**: Provides a standard grid showing the entire month. This is best for users who are booking far in advance and need to see high-level availability.

<Frame>
  ![Month view layout](/images/appearance/appearance-6.png)
</Frame>

**Weekly View**: Displays a side-by-side comparison of days within a specific week. This is ideal for users looking for an immediate opening within the next few days.

<Frame>
  ![Weekly view layout](/images/appearance/appearance-7.png)
</Frame>

**Column View**: A sleek, vertical list of dates followed by their specific time slots. This is the most mobile-friendly view and is great for quick selection.

<Frame>
  ![Column view layout](/images/appearance/appearance-8.png)
</Frame>

### Enabling multiple views

By checking the boxes for **Month**, **Weekly**, and **Column**, you allow your bookers to switch between these layouts manually using buttons on your public page.

<Frame>
  ![Enable multiple layout views](/images/appearance/appearance-9.png)
</Frame>

If you only want your users to see one specific style, uncheck the other two.

<Frame>
  ![Single layout view enabled](/images/appearance/appearance-10.png)
</Frame>

### Setting the default view

Under the **Default view** header, you will see buttons for Month, Weekly, and Column. Select the one you want to load automatically when someone clicks your booking link.

<Frame>
  ![Set default booking view](/images/appearance/appearance-11.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  To provide the most flexibility, check all three layout boxes (**Month**, **Weekly**, and **Column**). This allows your bookers to switch to the view they find most comfortable while navigating your schedule.
</Tip>
