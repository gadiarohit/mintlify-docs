Integrating Google Analytics (GA) and Google Tag Manager (GTM) allows you to track visitor behavior on your booking pages, measure conversion rates, and analyze the effectiveness of your marketing campaigns.

<Info>
  Before you start, have your tracking ID ready — a Google Analytics **Measurement ID** (`G-XXXXXXX`, or a legacy `UA-XXXXXX-Y`) or a Google Tag Manager **container ID** (`GTM-XXXXXX`). You'll paste it into Cal ID during setup.
</Info>

## Accessing the app store

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Open your main dashboard at [Cal ID](https://cal.id/home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Apps">
    Click on [**Apps**](https://cal.id/apps) in the left-hand sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by category">
    Choose **Analytics** from the featured categories at the top to quickly find tracking tools.

    <Frame>
      ![Analytics category in the App store](/images/apps/google-analytics-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting up Google Analytics

Google Analytics helps you understand how visitors find and interact with your booking page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate and install Google Analytics">
    Find the **Google Analytics** card within the Analytics category and click the **Install** button on the card.

    <Frame>
      ![Google Analytics card with Install button](/images/apps/google-analytics-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose account">
    Select the specific account where you want to install the integration.

    <Frame>
      ![Account selection for Google Analytics](/images/apps/google-analytics-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select event types">
    Choose the specific **Event Types** you wish to track, or select **Set up later** if you prefer to configure this at a different time.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting event types to track](/images/apps/google-analytics-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Tracking ID">
    If you selected event types, a pop-up will appear asking for your **Tracking ID** (formatted as `UA-XXXXXX-Y` or `G-XXXXXXX`).

    <Frame>
      ![Entering the Google Analytics tracking ID](/images/apps/google-analytics-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** button to activate tracking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where to find your tracking IDs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Google Analytics">
    Open your Google Analytics account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Admin">
    Click **Admin** (gear icon) in the bottom left.

    <Frame>
      ![Google Analytics Admin gear icon](/images/apps/google-analytics-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Data Streams">
    Under the Data Collection and modification column, click **Data Streams**.

    <Frame>
      ![Data Streams in Google Analytics](/images/apps/google-analytics-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your data stream">
    Select your website data stream and copy the Measurement ID, or if you do not see any data stream, create one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Measurement ID">
    Copy your **MEASUREMENT ID** from the top right corner and paste it in the Tracking ID field.

    <Frame>
      ![Copying the Measurement ID](/images/apps/google-analytics-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to set up in Google Analytics

To get the most out of your data, configure the following in your Google Analytics dashboard:

- **Create a Property**: Ensure you have a property created for your booking website.
- **Set up Events**: Define custom events to track specific actions, such as **Booking Form View**, **Booking Form Submission**, and **Payment Completed**.
- **Conversion Goals**: Set up goals based on these events to measure your conversion rate.

## Setting up Google Tag Manager

Google Tag Manager allows you to manage and deploy marketing tags without modifying code directly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Locate and install Google Tag Manager">
    Find the **Google Tag Manager** card in the App Store and click the **Install** button.

    <Frame>
      ![Google Tag Manager card with Install button](/images/apps/google-analytics-9.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose account">
    Select your preferred account for installation.

    <Frame>
      ![Account selection for Google Tag Manager](/images/apps/google-analytics-10.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select event types">
    Pick the event types to be tracked by GTM.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting event types for GTM](/images/apps/google-analytics-11.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Tracking ID">
    Enter your **Tracking ID** (formatted as `GTM-XXXXXX`) in the pop-up field.

    <Frame>
      ![Entering the GTM tracking ID](/images/apps/google-analytics-12.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** button to integrate GTM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to set up in Google Tag Manager

Once integrated, use GTM to manage your tracking:

- **Tags**: Create tags for Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or other conversion tracking tools.
- **Triggers**: Set triggers for when these tags should fire (e.g., when a user lands on the booking success page).
- **Variables**: Use built-in variables to capture specific booking data like event duration or event type name.

## Verifying installation

After installing, you can verify if the tracking is working:

- **Real-time Reports**: Check the **Real-time** report in Google Analytics to see if your own visits are being tracked.
- **Tag Assistant**: Use a browser extension like Google Tag Assistant to confirm that the GTM container is loading correctly on your public booking page.

## How to add or change IDs for a specific event

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    From your main dashboard, click on the [**Event Types**](https://cal.id/event-types) tab in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and edit">
    Find the specific event you want to configure and click **Edit** to open its settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Apps">
    In the top navigation bar of the event editor, click on the **Apps** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle tracking tools">
    Locate **Google Analytics** and/or **Google Tag Manager** in the list. Switch the toggle to **ON** for the tool you want to use for this specific event.

    <Frame>
      ![Toggling tracking tools for an event type](/images/apps/google-analytics-13.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Tracking ID">
    For **Google Analytics**, paste your specific `G-XXXXXXX` or `UA-XXXXXX-Y` ID into the input field. For **Google Tag Manager**, paste your specific `GTM-XXXXXX` container ID into the input field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click the **Save** button in the top right corner to apply these unique tracking IDs to this event type.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Why use event-specific tracking?

- **Targeted Analytics**: Track conversions for high-value sales calls separately from general inquiries.
- **Clean Data**: Prevent data from different departments or teams from mixing in your Analytics reports.
- **Custom Triggers**: Fire specific marketing tags in GTM that only apply to certain services.
