Welcome to Cal ID, a free meeting-scheduling tool that takes the back-and-forth out of booking time. Instead of trading emails to find a slot that works, you share one personal link and let people pick from the times you're actually free.

Behind that link, Cal ID connects to your calendar, checks your real availability, and confirms bookings automatically. You decide the meeting types you offer, the hours you're open, and how far in advance people can reserve time, and every confirmed booking lands on your calendar with the meeting details already attached.
