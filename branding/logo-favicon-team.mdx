The Custom brand logo and favicon section allows you to fully own your booking experience by replacing the Cal ID branding with your official organization logo and browser icon.

## Step-by-step configuration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Teams">
    Click on the **Teams** in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Team Menu">
    Locate the team, click the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Edit team**.

    <Frame>
      ![Edit team menu](/images/branding/logo-favicon-team-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Custom Branding Tab">
    Click on the **Custom Branding** tab at the top of the page.

    <Frame>
      ![Custom Branding tab](/images/branding/logo-favicon-team-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable the Feature">
    Scroll to Custom brand logo and favicon and switch the section toggle to **ON**.

    <Frame>
      ![Enable custom brand logo and favicon](/images/branding/logo-favicon-team-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Brand Logo">
    - Click the **Upload logo** button.
    - Select your **600 x 400 px** image.

    <Frame>
      ![Upload brand logo](/images/branding/logo-favicon-team-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Brand Favicon">
    - Click the **Upload favicon** button.
    - Select your icon file (ensure it is under **1 MB**).

    <Frame>
      ![Upload brand favicon](/images/branding/logo-favicon-team-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the Update button (if visible) to apply the changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Visible changes for your team public booking page

- **Public Booking Page**: Your custom logo will appear clearly at the bottom of your booking interface, replacing the default branding.

<Frame>
  ![Custom logo on team public booking page](/images/branding/logo-favicon-team-6.png)
</Frame>

- **Browser Tab**: Your uploaded favicon will be displayed in the browser tab whenever a guest opens your booking link.

<Frame>
  ![Custom favicon in browser tab](/images/branding/logo-favicon-team-7.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  For the **Brand Logo**, using a file with a transparent background (like a .PNG) is highly recommended. This ensures your logo looks natural whether your guest is viewing your page in **Light** or **Dark** mode.
</Tip>
