This document explains how to add new team members to a team that has already been created.

## Method 1: Direct invitation from Teams list

1. **Log in to Cal ID**: Access your account dashboard.
2. **Go to Teams**: Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
3. **Open Invitation Menu**: Locate the team, click on the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Invite team member**.

<Frame>
  ![Invite team member option from the Teams list](/images/teams/invite-members-1.png)
</Frame>

4. **Enter Details**: Input the email address of the new member and select their role (**Admin** or **Member**).
5. **Send Invite**: Click **Send invite**.

<Frame>
  ![Entering member email and role](/images/teams/invite-members-2.png)
</Frame>

## Method 2: Invitation from team settings

1. **Log in to Cal ID**: Access your account dashboard.
2. **Go to Teams**: Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
3. **Open Edit Page**: Go to the **Teams** page, click the **three horizontal dots** for the team, and select **Edit team**.

<Frame>
  ![Edit team option](/images/teams/invite-members-3.png)
</Frame>

4. **Go to Members Tab**: Click on the **Members** tab at the top of the page.
5. **Add Member**: Click the blue **\+ Add team member** button in the top right.

<Frame>
  ![Add team member button in the Members tab](/images/teams/invite-members-4.png)
</Frame>

6. **Enter Details**: Enter the email address and select the role.

<Frame>
  ![Entering member details](/images/teams/invite-members-5.png)
</Frame>

7. **Send Invite**: Click **Send invitation**.

<Note>
  Only **Owners** or **Admins** can invite new members to a team.
</Note>
