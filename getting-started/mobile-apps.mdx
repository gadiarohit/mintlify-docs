Stay connected and manage your bookings on the go with the Cal ID mobile application. Our mobile apps provide a seamless experience, allowing you to view your schedule, share booking links, and manage event types directly from your smartphone.

## Download the App

You can download the official Cal ID app for your device using the links below:

- **For Android**: [Download on Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=id.cal.android&authuser=1)
- **For iOS (iPhone/iPad)**: [Download on Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cal-id-by-onehash/id6747376362)

## Key features on mobile

- **Real-time Notifications**: Receive instant alerts for new bookings, cancellations, and rescheduled meetings.
- **Quick Share Links**: Copy and share your event type links via WhatsApp, Slack, email, or any other messaging app with just a few taps.
- **Availability Management**: Toggle your event types on or off and update your schedules even when you are away from your desk.
- **Booking Overview**: Access a clean, organized list of all your upcoming and past bookings in one view.
- **Mobile-Optimized Interface**: A fast and intuitive dashboard designed specifically for a great mobile experience.
