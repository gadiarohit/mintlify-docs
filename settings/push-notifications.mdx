This document explains how to configure push notifications to receive real-time alerts on your browser for booking activities.

## Steps to enable push notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click on your profile icon and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Push Notifications page">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow browser notifications">
    Click on the **Allow Browser Notifications** button.
  </Step>
</Steps>
