Follow these steps if you already have a **WhatsApp Business app** installed on your mobile device and want to link it to Cal ID.

<Info>
  Your address and Tax ID must be verified to trigger the message.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the connection method">
    From the integration screen, click **Connect WhatsApp Business app**. This opens the connection process for your existing app.

    <Frame>
      ![Connect WhatsApp Business app option](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your phone number">
    Type the phone number associated with your WhatsApp Business app, then click **Next**. Only **verified phone numbers** are shown here.

    <Frame>
      ![Entering the WhatsApp Business phone number](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your access code">
    Click **Use an access code instead** to generate a secure access code, and note it down.

    <Frame>
      ![Using an access code instead](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code on your mobile device">
    On your phone:

    1. Open the **WhatsApp Business app**.
    2. Go to **Menu** or **Settings** by tapping the menu icon.
    3. Select **Account**.
    4. Select **Business platform**.
    5. Enter the **access code** you copied earlier.
    6. Click **Next**.
    7. Paste the generated code.

    <Frame>
      ![Entering the access code in the WhatsApp Business app](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter additional data">
    Complete any additional details requested, then click **Next**.

    <Frame>
      ![Filling in additional required information](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the connection">
    Review all details to check that everything is correct, then click **Confirm**.

    <Frame>
      ![Confirming the connection setup](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the QR code">
    A QR code page appears. Scan the code with your mobile device and follow the on-screen instructions to complete any additional steps.

    <Frame>
      ![Scanning the QR code to complete the connection](/images/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your WhatsApp Business app is connected to Cal ID and ready to send workflow messages.
</Check>
