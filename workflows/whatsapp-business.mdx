## How to integrate WhatsApp Business with Cal ID?

Cal ID and WhatsApp integration lets you trigger notifications and reminders via your own business number.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install WhatsApp Business and connect">
    - **Log in to Cal ID.**
    - **Go to Apps Section**
    - **Search for WhatsApp Business and install it.**

    **Select Account Type - Choose whether to install on:**

    - **Personal account (for individual use)**
    - **Team account (for team collaboration)**

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting the account type for WhatsApp Business](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-1.png)
    </Frame>

    Page will appear asking you to Connect WhatsApp Business. Click on this option to proceed.

    <Frame>
      ![Connect WhatsApp Business prompt](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-2.png)
    </Frame>

    **Once you see the confirmation screen, click on Continue to move to the next step.**

    <Frame>
      ![Confirmation screen with Continue button](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-3.png)
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      If you already have a WhatsApp Business account: Start with the next step.

      If you want to create a new WhatsApp Business account refer [Create a new WhatsApp Business Account](/workflows/create-whatsapp-account).

      If you want to connect an existing WhatsApp Business app: [Connect with WhatsApp Business App](/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app).
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your business portfolio">
    **Choose Your Business Portfolio** - Select the business portfolio associated with your WhatsApp Business account

    **Select WhatsApp Business Account** - Pick the WhatsApp number you want to use

    <Note>
      Your address and Tax ID must be verified on Meta to trigger the templates.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
      ![Choosing a business portfolio and WhatsApp Business account](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your phone number">
    **Select Your Phone Number** - Choose the phone number from the list that you want to use with WhatsApp Business

    **Click on "Next"** - Proceed to the confirmation step

    This phone number will be the main contact point for your WhatsApp Business integration.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting the WhatsApp Business phone number](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the information">
    Review the Information

    **Click on "Confirm"** - Verify and confirm your settings

    This step ensures that everything is set up correctly before finalizing the connection.

    <Frame>
      ![Reviewing and confirming the integration settings](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the workflow">
    After you click Confirm, your WhatsApp Business app is successfully installed and connected to your Cal ID account.

    Next, go to Cal ID and choose the event type where you want to set the workflow. Select your WhatsApp Business phone number, choose the template from the Message Template dropdown and save the workflow.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting the WhatsApp Business number and template in a workflow](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-7.png)
    </Frame>

    If you do not have any template, click on Create Template and create one.

    <Frame>
      ![Create Template option for WhatsApp workflows](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
