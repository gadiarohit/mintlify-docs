Your Profile page is where you manage your personal identity and contact information on Cal ID. These details define how you appear to others on your public booking page.

## How to access profile settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click on the **Settings** icon in the bottom left of your sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Profile">
    Ensure you are on the **Profile** tab under the personal settings section.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key profile features

### Profile picture

- **Update Avatar**: Click **Upload Avatar** (at most 5 MB in size) to choose a professional photo from your computer.
- **Remove**: If you wish to delete your current photo, click the **Remove** button.

### Username and public URL

- **Username**: You can edit your username in the text field. This directly changes your public booking link (e.g., `cal.id/your-username`).
- **Impact**: Changing this will break any old links you have shared, so be sure to update your website or email signature.

<Frame>
  ![Username and public URL field](/images/settings/profile-2.png)
</Frame>

### Personal information

- **Full Name**: Enter the name you want displayed on your booking page and in calendar invitations.
- **About (Bio)**: Use the text editor to write a brief bio. You can use **Bold**, **Italics**, or add links to provide more context about who you are to your bookers.

<Frame>
  ![Personal information fields](/images/settings/profile-3.png)
</Frame>

### Email management

- **Primary Email**: Your main email address is marked with a "Primary" tag.
- **Add New Email**: Click the **\+ Add Email** button to link additional email addresses to your account.
- **Manage Emails**: You can click the three dots next to any secondary email to make it primary or remove it.

<Frame>
  ![Email management options](/images/settings/profile-4.png)
</Frame>

### Phone number and WhatsApp

- **Add/Change Number**: Select your country code from the dropdown and enter your mobile number.
- **Validation**: The system will display a "Number is valid" badge once it confirms the format is correct.
- **WhatsApp Integration**: Check the box **"Allow bookers to reach you on WhatsApp via this phone number"** if you want to enable direct messaging for your clients.

<Frame>
  ![Phone number and WhatsApp settings](/images/settings/profile-5.png)
</Frame>

### Connected accounts

- This section shows which external accounts (like Google) are currently connected for login or calendar syncing.
- You can click **Disconnect** if you wish to remove an account's access.

<Frame>
  ![Connected accounts section](/images/settings/profile-6.png)
</Frame>

## Saving your changes

After making any updates to your profile, click the **Update** button at the bottom of the page to ensure your public profile and settings are synchronized.

## Danger zone: delete account

If you wish to permanently close your Cal ID account, you can do so from the bottom of the Profile page.

- **Permanent Action**: Please be careful, as **account deletion cannot be undone**.
- **How to Delete**: Scroll to the very bottom of your profile settings to find the **Danger zone** and click the **Delete account** button.

<Frame>
  ![Danger zone delete account](/images/settings/profile-7.png)
</Frame>
