The **Settings** tab lets Team Owners and Admins control internal privacy, administrative access, and booking limits for the whole team from one place.

<Info>
  These controls are available to **Team Owners** and **Admins** only. Regular members won't see the Settings tab.
</Info>

## Access team settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Open your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Teams">
    Click the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your team">
    Locate your team, click the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Edit team**.

    <Frame>
      ![Edit team option](/images/teams/advanced-settings-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the **Settings** tab at the top.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available settings

| Setting | What it does | When toggled ON |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **User impersonation** | Lets Owners/Admins log in temporarily as another member | Admins can access a member's interface to view config or make changes on their behalf |
| **Private team** | Hides the team roster from non-admin members | Members can't see each other; they see "Only admins can see members of this team." |
| **Limit booking frequency** | Caps how many appointments a member can take in a timeframe | Enter a count and interval to enforce the restriction |

## Feature breakdown

### User impersonation

Allows the **Owners** or **Admins** of your team to log in temporarily as another member. When toggled **ON**, administrative roles can access a member's Cal ID interface to view their configuration or make necessary changes on their behalf.

<Frame>
  ![User Impersonation toggle](/images/teams/advanced-settings-2.png)
</Frame>

### Private team

Hides the team roster from non-administrative members. When toggled **ON**:

- Team members will not be able to see each other.
- Members who attempt to view the roster will see a message stating: **"Only admins can see members of this team."**

<Frame>
  ![Private Team toggle](/images/teams/advanced-settings-3.png)
</Frame>

### Limit booking frequency

Prevents your team from being overbooked by limiting how many appointments a user can schedule within a specific timeframe. Toggle the switch to **ON** to activate the restriction.

<Frame>
  ![Limit Booking Frequency toggle](/images/teams/advanced-settings-4.png)
</Frame>

- **Set the Count**: Enter a number (e.g., **1**) to define the maximum bookings allowed.
- **Set the Interval**: Choose the timeframe from the dropdown menu (e.g., **Per day**, **Per week**, or **Per month**).
- **Multiple Limits**: Click **\+ Add Limit** to set tiered restrictions (for example, 1 booking per day AND 3 bookings per week).

<Tip>
  Combine multiple limits to protect both daily focus time and weekly capacity at once.
</Tip>
