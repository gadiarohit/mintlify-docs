Connecting WhatsApp Business to Cal ID lets your workflow reminders and confirmations reach guests from your own official business number instead of the default Cal ID number. This page is the hub for the full integration; the steps below walk you through installing the app, choosing your number, and attaching it to a workflow.

## Integrate WhatsApp Business with Cal ID

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install WhatsApp Business and connect">
    - Log in to Cal ID.
    - Go to the **Apps** section.
    - Search for **WhatsApp Business** and install it.

    Choose the account type to install on:

    - **Personal account** — for individual use.
    - **Team account** — for team collaboration.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting the account type for WhatsApp Business](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-1.png)
    </Frame>

    A page appears asking you to connect WhatsApp Business. Click it to proceed.

    <Frame>
      ![Connect WhatsApp Business prompt](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-2.png)
    </Frame>

    On the confirmation screen, click **Continue** to move to the next step.

    <Frame>
      ![Confirmation screen with Continue button](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-3.png)
    </Frame>

    <Info>
      Pick the path that matches your situation:

      - **Already have a WhatsApp Business account?** Continue with the next step below.
      - **Need to create a new account?** See [Create a new WhatsApp Business account](/workflows/create-whatsapp-account).
      - **Connecting an existing WhatsApp Business app?** See [Connect with WhatsApp Business app](/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app).
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your business portfolio">
    Select the **business portfolio** associated with your WhatsApp Business account, then pick the **WhatsApp Business account** (the number) you want to use.

    <Info>
      Your address and Tax ID must be verified on Meta to trigger the templates.
    </Info>

    <Frame>
      ![Choosing a business portfolio and WhatsApp Business account](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your phone number">
    Choose the phone number from the list you want to use with WhatsApp Business, then click **Next** to proceed to the confirmation step. This number becomes the main contact point for your WhatsApp Business integration.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting the WhatsApp Business phone number](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the information">
    Review the information and click **Confirm** to verify your settings. This ensures everything is set up correctly before finalizing the connection.

    <Frame>
      ![Reviewing and confirming the integration settings](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up the workflow">
    After you click Confirm, your WhatsApp Business app is installed and connected to your Cal ID account.

    Next, go to Cal ID and choose the event type where you want the workflow. Select your WhatsApp Business phone number, choose the template from the **Message Template** dropdown, and save the workflow.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting the WhatsApp Business number and template in a workflow](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-7.png)
    </Frame>

    If you don't have a template yet, click **Create Template** and create one.

    <Frame>
      ![Create Template option for WhatsApp workflows](/images/workflows/whatsapp-business-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  WhatsApp Business is connected and attached to your workflow. Reminders will now be sent from your own business number.
</Check>
