Add a new integration to your account in a few clicks from the Cal ID App Store. We suggest firstly adding a calendar integration, then optionally a video conferencing platform, and then try out the other apps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Apps page">
    Head over to the **Apps** page, where you'll find our app store, and find the app you'd like to install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Click **Install** on the right of the app's card to add it right away, or click the card to view more details. In the details page you can read more about each app, then click **Install** to add it to your account.

    <Frame>
      ![App details page with Install button](/images/apps/install-app-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate if prompted">
    If the app requires authentication, for instance to connect your Google Account, a dialog will be opened prompting you to authenticate. Once complete, the app will be successfully installed to your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  After installing, enable the app on the specific event types where you want to use it from each event's **Apps** tab.
</Tip>
