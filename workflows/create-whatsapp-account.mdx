If you don't yet have a WhatsApp Business account, create one in Meta first. This gives you the business portfolio and account you'll connect to Cal ID for automated booking messages.

<Info>
  You must verify your Tax ID on Meta to trigger the templates. Your Meta business and address details need to be verified before templates will send.
</Info>

## Create a new WhatsApp Business account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the portfolio and account">
    In the respective fields, select **Create a business portfolio** and **Create a WhatsApp Business account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the setup">
    Fill in all required fields and save.

    <Frame>
      ![Creating a new business portfolio and WhatsApp Business account](/images/workflows/create-whatsapp-account-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your WhatsApp Business account is ready. You can now connect it with Cal ID — see [Connect with WhatsApp Business app](/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app).
</Check>
