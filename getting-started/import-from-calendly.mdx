The **Import** tool lets you bring your existing Calendly setup into Cal ID in one move — your **Event Types, Availability, and all Bookings** transfer directly into your account, so you can switch platforms without rebuilding anything.

<Info>
  Before you start, make sure you have your Calendly login handy — you'll authorize the connection partway through, and the import pulls data straight from your live Calendly account.
</Info>

## Step-by-step migration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Settings page">
    Open the **Settings** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Import">
    Click the **Import** option in the left-hand sidebar, under the **Others** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage notifications">
    Locate the **Import from calendly** section. Check the box for **Notify past bookers about your migration** if you want to automatically inform your previous clients about your move to Cal ID.

    <Frame>
      ![Import from Calendly section with the notify past bookers option](/images/getting-started/import-from-calendly-1.png)
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      Deciding whether to notify past bookers is a one-time choice made at import. Set this option the way you want it **before** you connect and import your data.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and import">
    Click the blue **Connect Calendly** button in the top-right corner to connect your account and bring your data over to Cal ID.

    <Frame>
      ![Connect Calendly button in the top-right corner](/images/getting-started/import-from-calendly-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your Calendly data is now in Cal ID. Your event types, availability, and bookings have all made the move.
</Check>
