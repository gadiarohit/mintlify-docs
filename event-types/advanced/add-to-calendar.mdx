Choose exactly which connected calendar stores a specific event type's bookings, so work meetings, side projects, and personal time each land where they belong.

By default, Cal ID adds new bookings to your **Primary** calendar. The **Add Events to Calendar** feature (under the **Advanced** tab) overrides that default per event type, keeping your professional and personal lives cleanly separated.

## Why use "Add Events to Calendar"?

- **Organization:** If you keep separate calendars for "Work," "Side Projects," and "Personal," you can ensure each booking lands exactly where it belongs.
- **Team management:** In a team environment, direct specific event types to a shared team calendar rather than your private one.
- **Visibility:** See your availability at a glance by ensuring your most relevant calendar is the one being populated with new meetings.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    Click on **Event Types** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an event">
    Click the specific event you want to customize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced">
    Click on the **Advanced** tab in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate Add Events to Calendar">
    Look for the dropdown menu labeled **Add events to calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your calendar">
    Select the specific connected calendar (e.g., Google, Outlook, or Apple) where you want these bookings to appear.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** button at the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- **Automatic creation:** Once a guest finishes booking, Cal ID immediately pushes the event details to the calendar you selected.
- **Conflict checking:** Even when you choose a specific calendar to **add** events to, Cal ID still checks **all** your connected calendars for conflicts so you don't get double-booked.
- **Independence:** Changing this setting for one event type (like "Project Kickoff") will not affect where your other event types (like "Quick Chat") are stored.

<Tip>
  To set a global default for **all** event types, head to your **`/settings/calendars`**. From there, you can choose one primary calendar that's used whenever an event-specific one isn't chosen.
</Tip>
