Your **Installed Apps** page is the central hub for the tools and integrations you've already added to Cal ID — view them, filter by category, adjust their settings, or disconnect the ones you no longer need.

## Steps to view installed apps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Open your account dashboard at [Cal ID](https://cal.id/home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Apps">
    Click on the [**Apps**](https://cal.id/apps) icon in the left-hand sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Installed Apps">
    Click on the **Installed Apps** tab at the top of the page.

    <Frame>
      ![Installed Apps tab in Cal ID](/images/apps/manage-installed-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing your apps

The **Installed Apps** section is your central hub for managing your active integrations:

- **Filter by Category**: Once you are on the Installed Apps page, you will see various categories (such as Calendar, Conferencing, or Payment). Click on a specific category to see only the installed apps within that group.

<Frame>
  ![Filtering installed apps by category](/images/apps/manage-installed-2.png)
</Frame>

- **Manage App Settings**: Click on any app in the list to adjust its specific configurations, such as choosing which calendars to check for conflicts or disconnecting the app entirely.
- **Set Default Locations**: If you have multiple video conferencing apps installed, you can use this section to set your preferred one as the default for new meetings.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting or uninstalling an app also removes it from any event types that rely on it. For example, uninstalling a video conferencing app can leave those events without a meeting location, and disconnecting a calendar stops Cal ID from checking it for conflicts. Review where an app is used before removing it.
</Warning>
