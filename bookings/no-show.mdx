In **Cal ID**, you can maintain accurate meeting records by marking guests as "No-Show" for past appointments where attendees failed to appear. This ensures your booking history reflects reality and allows for better follow-up management.

Tracking attendance helps you distinguish between successful meetings and missed sessions directly from your dashboard.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

| **Step** | **Action** | **Description** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Navigate to Past Bookings** | Go to the **Bookings** tab and select the **Past** sub-tab to see completed meetings. |
| **2** | **Expand Booking Details** | Click the **Details** arrow on the specific booking you wish to manage. |
| **3** | **Mark as No-Show** | Click the **Mark no-show** button located in the bottom-right corner. |
| **4** | **Unmark if Necessary** | If a mistake was made, click **Unmark no-show** in the same location to revert the status. |

## Handling Multiple Attendees ('n' more)

For meetings with more than two attendees, Cal ID provides a streamlined way to manage group attendance:

- **Open the Checklist**: Click on the **Mark as no show** button, which will trigger a checklist popup for meetings with multiple participants.

<Frame>
  ![No-show checklist popup for multiple attendees](/images/bookings/no-show-2.png)
</Frame>

- **Bulk Management**: Use this checklist to mark or unmark multiple guests as "No-Show" simultaneously.

<Frame>
  ![Bulk marking attendees as no-show](/images/bookings/no-show-3.png)
</Frame>

- **Accurate Records**: This feature ensures that even in large group sessions, you can accurately record which specific individuals were present.

## Attendance Management Overview

- **Filter View**: Use the **Past** filter to isolate meetings that have already occurred.
- **Attendee Information**: You can view the attendee's name, timezone, and email address within the details section.

<Tip>
  **Professional Follow-ups:** By marking a guest as a no-show, you keep your data clean for reporting. This status can also be used as a trigger for automated workflows, such as sending a personalized email to the guest asking them to reschedule.
</Tip>
