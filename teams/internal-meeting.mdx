In **Cal ID**, the **Internal Team Meeting** feature allows you to quickly find common free time among your colleagues and book a meeting without ever leaving your dashboard.

## Scheduling an internal team meeting

Unlike a public booking link where others book you, this feature is designed for you to proactively schedule a meeting with your own team members based on their real-time availability.

## How to schedule a meeting

1. **Login** to your Cal ID account and navigate to the **Availability** page in the sidebar.
2. Click on the **Team Availability** tab.

<Frame>
  ![Team Availability tab on the Availability page](/images/teams/internal-meeting-1.png)
</Frame>

3. **Select Members**: You will see a list of your team members. Check the boxes next to the specific members you want to include in the meeting.

<Frame>
  ![Selecting team members for the meeting](/images/teams/internal-meeting-2.png)
</Frame>

4. **Find Common Ground**: After selecting the members, click the **Book Members** button.

<Frame>
  ![Book Members button](/images/teams/internal-meeting-3.png)
</Frame>

5. **View Available Slots**: A popup window will appear showing a calendar. This calendar **only** shows the time slots where **all** selected members are simultaneously free (common availability).

<Frame>
  ![Calendar showing common availability slots](/images/teams/internal-meeting-4.png)
</Frame>

6. **Book the Time**:
   - Select the date and the specific time slot that works for everyone.
   - Fill out the booking questions (Meeting title, description, etc.).
   - Click **Confirm**.

## What happens next?

Once you hit confirm, the system handles the rest of the communication:

- **Calendar Sync**: The meeting is automatically added to the personal calendars of every selected team member.
- **Email Notifications**: All selected members immediately receive an email invitation with the meeting details, location link, and time.
- **Centralized Record**: The meeting will also appear in your **Bookings** tab within Cal ID for easy management.

<Tip>
  **Quick Syncs**: This is the fastest way to schedule emergency meetings or internal reviews. Instead of messaging everyone asking "Are you free at 2 PM?", the **Team Availability** tab gives you the answer instantly.
</Tip>
