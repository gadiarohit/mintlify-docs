In **Cal ID**, the **Private Links** feature offers a layer of security and exclusivity for your scheduling by allowing you to generate unique, restricted URLs that do not expose your main profile.

## Using Private Links for Maximum Privacy

While standard event types have public URLs associated with your username, Private Links allow you to invite attendees without sharing your direct booking page path.

## Why use Private Links?

- **Protect Your Username:** This feature prevents attendees from seeing your main **Cal ID** username, which otherwise leads directly to all your public booking options.
- **Single-Use Security:** You can generate a link that regenerates or expires after each use, ensuring an attendee cannot reuse the same URL to book your time repeatedly.
- **Control Distribution:** It prevents your link from being shared around unintentionally, helping you better manage incoming booking requests and maintain a clean schedule.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced">
    Go to **Event Types**, select your event, and click the **Advanced** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Private Links">
    Toggle the **Private Links** switch to **ON**.

    <Frame>
      ![Private Links toggle](/images/event-types/private-links-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Link Settings">
    Click the **Settings (gear icon)** next to the generated link.

    - Select **Usage-based expiration** and set it to **1 booking** to ensure the link regenerates and cannot be reused.

    <Frame>
      ![Usage-based expiration setting](/images/event-types/private-links-2.png)
    </Frame>

    - Alternatively, choose **Time-based expiration** if the link should only be active until a specific date.

    <Frame>
      ![Time-based expiration setting](/images/event-types/private-links-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy and Share">
    Use the **Copy** icon to grab the unique URL and share it with your attendee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** button at the top right to confirm your settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it Works for the Attendee

- The attendee sees a clean booking page that allows them to pick a time for that specific event.
- They do not see your public profile or other event types you may have.
- Once they complete the booking (if set to 1 use), the link will immediately become invalid for any future attempts.
