---
title: "Connect Apple Calendar with Cal ID"
description: "Step-by-step guide to connecting your Apple Calendar to Cal ID using an app-specific password."
icon: "calendar"
---

To connect Apple Calendar with Cal ID, please complete the following steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Apple Calendar app details">
    Go to [**Cal ID's App store**](https://cal.id/apps) and click on **Details** under the Apple Calendar card.

    <Frame>
      ![Apple Calendar card in the Cal ID App store](/images/apps/apple-calendar-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and open the setup page">
    Click on the **Install** button or, if you already have an Apple Calendar connection, click on the **Install** button. In the Apple Calendar connection setup page, click on the link and sign in to your Apple ID.

    <Frame>
      ![Apple Calendar connection setup page](/images/apps/apple-calendar-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open App-specific passwords">
    Once signed in and on the `https://appleid.apple.com/account/manage` page, click on **App-specific passwords**.

    <Frame>
      ![App-specific passwords option on Apple ID account page](/images/apps/apple-calendar-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new app-specific password">
    Now, click on the plus sign to add a new App-specific password to your account.

    <Frame>
      ![Add a new app-specific password](/images/apps/apple-calendar-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name and copy the password">
    Next, provide a name for your new App-specific password. You should now have a new app-specific password showing on the screen. Simply copy this password.

    <Frame>
      ![Generated app-specific password](/images/apps/apple-calendar-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials in Cal ID and save">
    Now enter your Apple ID and the newly generated app-specific password (you copied this in the previous step) in the Cal ID [Apple Calendar Setup](https://cal.id/apps/apple-calendar/setup) page. Finally, click on **Save**. You should now be redirected to the `https://cal.id/apps/installed/calendar` page showing your recently added Apple Calendar.

    <Frame>
      ![Installed Apple Calendar in Cal ID](/images/apps/apple-calendar-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it. Your Apple Calendar is now connected to your **Cal ID** account.
