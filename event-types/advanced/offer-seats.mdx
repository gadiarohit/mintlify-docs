In **Cal ID**, the **Offer seats** feature allows you to transform a one-on-one meeting into a **group session**. This is perfect for **webinars**, classes, or **workshops** where multiple people need to book the same time slot.

## Setting up offer seats

This setting allows you to define how many people can attend a single event at the same time.

## Why use Offer Seats?

- **Host Group Sessions:** Easily manage classes, webinars, or team training where several people join at once.
- **Maximize Efficiency:** Instead of repeating the same information five times, you can speak to five people in one hour.
- **Automatic Status:** Once all the seats are filled, the system automatically hides that time slot so it cannot be overbooked.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    Click on **Event Types** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Event">
    Click on the specific event you want to turn into a group session.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced">
    Click on the **Advanced** tab in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Offer Seats">
    Locate the **Offer seats** section at the bottom of the page and toggle the switch to **ON**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Seat Count">
    Enter the maximum number of people allowed to book that specific time slot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** button at the top right.

    <Frame>
      ![Offer seats settings](/images/event-types/offer-seats-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works for the booker

- The first person to book selects the time slot as usual.
- The time slot **remains visible** on your booking page for others until the maximum number of "seats" has been filled.

<Frame>
  ![Booking page showing available seats](/images/event-types/offer-seats-2.png)
</Frame>

- If you have a meeting with 20 seats and 13 are taken, the guest might see a message indicating how many spots are left.

## Important Details

- **Individual Confirmations:** Each person who books a seat will receive their own confirmation email and calendar invite.
- **Availability:** The slot will only disappear from your calendar once the **total number of seats** is reached or if you manually mark yourself as busy during that time.
