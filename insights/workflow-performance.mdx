Keep your automation healthy. The **Workflows** insight tab lets you confirm that reminders, follow-ups, and notifications are being delivered reliably to your guests, and catch failures before they cost you a booking.

## Open the Workflows dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your dashboard at [Cal ID](https://cal.id/home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Insights">
    Click **Insights** in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Workflows tab">
    Click the **Workflows** tab at the top of the dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key metrics and reports

### Execution scorecards

These scorecards summarize how your automations performed across the selected date range.

| Metric | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Triggered** | The total number of times an automation was kicked off by an event. |
| **Workflows Queued** | Number of workflows that are in queue and will be delivered. |
| **Workflows Delivered** | The number of Emails, SMS, or WhatsApp messages successfully sent. |
| **Workflows Read** | The number of workflow messages that recipients have opened. |
| **Workflows Failed** | Automations that did not complete (often due to invalid contact info or integration errors). |
| **Workflows Cancelled** | Actions that were scheduled but stopped (e.g., a reminder cancelled because the meeting was cancelled or deleted). |

## Filtering and account selection

<Info>
  Both filters apply to every scorecard and graph on the page. Set them before reading the numbers so you're comparing the right period and account.
</Info>

- **Date Range**: Use the top-right filter to see how many automations ran during a specific campaign or month.
- **Team vs. Personal**: Switch the account filter at the top left to see if team-wide workflows are performing differently than your personal ones.
