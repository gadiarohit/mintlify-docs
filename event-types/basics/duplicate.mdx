The **Duplicate** feature makes an exact copy of an event type you already set up, so you can launch variations without rebuilding everything from scratch.

## Why use it?

- **Save time:** If you have a 30-minute meeting with perfect "Booking Questions" and "Reminders," you don't have to type them all again for a 60-minute version. Just copy it!
- **Stay consistent:** It copies everything (like your availability and reminders) so you don't forget any important details.
- **Create a template:** You can make one "perfect" meeting and just copy it whenever you need a new one.

## How to do it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Login to your [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home) account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Event Types">
    Navigate to the **Event Types** tab in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Duplicate">
    Find the event you want to copy, click the **three dots (···)** on the right side, and select **Duplicate**.

    <Frame>
      ![Duplicate option in menu](/images/event-types/duplicate-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the details">
    A **Duplicate Event Type** box will pop up. Here you can quickly update:

    - **Title**: Give your new event a clear name.
    - **URL**: Customize the link for this specific copy.
    - **Description**: Edit the text your guests will see.
    - **Duration**: Set how long the new meeting should last.

    <Frame>
      ![Duplicate Event Type box](/images/event-types/duplicate-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Click **Continue** to create the new event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    Your new event will now appear in your list. **Changes to this new event will not affect your original event**.

    <Frame>
      ![New duplicated event in the list](/images/event-types/duplicate-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
