The public page banner is the first thing guests see—a wide, professional image that sits at the very top of your booking page.

<Info>
  Your banner image must be exactly **3200 x 320 px** and **1 MB** or smaller.
</Info>

## Step-by-step configuration

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Custom Branding">
    Select the **Custom Branding** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Banner section">
    Find the **Public page banner** section at the top of the page.

    <Frame>
      ![Public page banner section](/images/branding/banner-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your image">
    Click the **Upload image** button, then select your file (ensure it meets the **3200 x 320** dimension and **1 MB** size limit).

    <Frame>
      ![Upload banner image](/images/branding/banner-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    The banner will display in the preview box once the upload is successful.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Because the banner is very wide and short, try to use a landscape photo or a brand pattern where the most important visual elements are centered. This prevents important details from being cut off on smaller mobile devices.
</Tip>
