Book a meeting on someone's Cal ID in a few quick steps: pick a time, share your details, and confirm your spot on the calendar.

<Note>
  Looking for your own link to send to a client? See [How to Find and Share Your Cal ID URL](/getting-started/share-your-url).
</Note>

## Book your meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking link">
    Open the Cal ID URL shared with you (e.g., `cal.id/username`). You'll see all available **Event Types** (such as "Consultation," "Discovery Call," or "Technical Support"). Click the **Schedule** button on the event you wish to book.

    <Frame>
      ![Public booking page listing available event types](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your date and time">
    - **Select a duration:** If the host allows multiple lengths for the meeting (e.g., 15m or 30m), select your preferred duration from the options on the left side of the page.
    - **Pick a date:** Select an available date from the calendar.
    - **Pick a time:** Choose a specific time slot that works for you.
    - **Adjust your timezone:** Cal ID **automatically** detects your location and sets the booking to your local timezone by default. You can manually change it using the timezone dropdown on the left to ensure perfect coordination.

    <Frame>
      ![Calendar with date, time, duration, and timezone options](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Provide your details">
    Answer the specific questions the host has included to help prepare for the meeting.

    - **Standard booking:** For free meetings, simply click the **Confirm** button.
    - **Payment (if applicable):** If the host has set a fee, click **Pay to Book** to complete your payment securely via the integrated payment gateway.

    <Frame>
      ![Booking details form with attendee questions](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-3.png)
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      ![Payment step for a paid booking](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm your booking">
    **Instant confirmation:** You'll immediately see a **"Booking Confirmed"** page, and the Meeting URL (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.) will be sent directly to your confirmation email and added to your calendar invite.

    <Frame>
      ![Booking Confirmed page](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-5.png)
    </Frame>

    <Info>
      **Requires confirmation:** If the host has enabled "Requires Confirmation," your booking is submitted as a request. You'll receive a final confirmation or rejection email once the admin reviews it. Once confirmed, your Meeting URL is sent to your confirmation email and added to your calendar invite.
    </Info>

    <Frame>
      ![Booking request submitted, awaiting host confirmation](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You're booked. Your spot is on the calendar and the meeting details are in your inbox.
</Check>

## Managing, cancelling, or rescheduling

Once your booking is finished, you can manage your appointment easily:

- **Two ways to manage:** You can reschedule or cancel your meeting directly from the **Booking Confirmation Page** (shown right after you book) or through the **Confirmation Email** you receive in your inbox.

<Frame>
  ![Reschedule and cancel options on the booking confirmation page](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-7.png)
</Frame>

- **How to do it:** Simply click the "Reschedule" or "Cancel" link provided in those locations to choose a new time or notify the host of a cancellation.

<Warning>
  To maintain a steady schedule, some hosts disable cancellation and rescheduling. If you don't see these options in your email or on the confirmation page, your booking is considered final, and you must contact the host directly for any changes.
</Warning>
