Event types are reusable meeting templates, like a "15-minute intro" or a "60-minute deep dive," that let guests book the right amount of time with you without any manual back-and-forth. Set them up once and share the link anywhere.

## What are event types?

Each event type is a pre-set meeting template you can share with anyone, fully customizable down to its name, URL, and duration.

- **Customizable settings**: Create as many as you need, each with its own unique availability and location (like Zoom or Google Meet and many more).
- **Smart availability**: When someone views your booking link, they only see slots where you are truly free. Cal ID automatically checks your integrated calendar for conflicts to prevent double-booking.
- **Professional examples**: An HR professional might have a "Job Interview" (30 min) and a "Performance Review" (60 min), each with different settings.

## How to create your event type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the new event">
    Log in to your [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home) account and navigate to the **Event Types** page, then click the **\+ New** button in the top right corner.

    <Frame>
      ![New event button](/images/event-types/overview-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the account type">
    - **Personal Event**: Set this up so people can book time directly with you.
    - **Team Event**: If you are part of a team, you can create an event type for that.

    <Note>
      If you aren't in a team, the event will automatically be added to your personal account.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
      ![Choose account type](/images/event-types/overview-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the basic setup">
    A pop-up will appear where you fill in the core details:

    - **Title**: Give your event a name (e.g., "Consultation"). This title automatically becomes part of the URL, but you can change it if you wish.
    - **Description**: Briefly explain what the meeting is for. This is shown to the booker on your public page.
    - **Duration**: Select how long the meeting will last (e.g., 15, 30, or 60 minutes).

    <Frame>
      ![Add new event type pop-up](/images/event-types/overview-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fine-tune the advanced configuration">
    After clicking **Continue**, a detailed page opens where you can fine-tune your event:

    - **Event Setup**: Edit your title, description, and location.
    - **Availability**: Set specific hours for this event type.
    - **Limits & Buffers**: Add extra time before or after meetings to give yourself a break.
    - **Advanced**: Adjust advanced settings.
    - **Workflows**: Set workflows for notifications and reminders.
    - **Recurring**: Set for repetitive bookings.
    - **Embed**: Get the embed code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Preview your booking page

Before sharing your link, you can easily see exactly what your Booker will see. There are two simple ways to view the preview:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the Event Types page">
    On your main dashboard, click on the **External Link icon** (square with an arrow) next to your event URL to open the live booking page in a new tab.

    <Frame>
      ![External link preview icon](/images/event-types/overview-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From the Edit Settings page">
    While you are inside editing an event, you can click the **Preview icon** located at the top right to see your changes in real-time.

    <Frame>
      ![Preview icon on edit page](/images/event-types/overview-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Key benefits

- **Saves Time**: No more "Are you free at 2 PM?" emails.
- **Flexibility**: Switch events on or off with a single toggle.
- **Easy Sharing**: Each event has its own unique link that you can send via email or embed on your website.
