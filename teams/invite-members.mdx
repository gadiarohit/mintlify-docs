Add new people to a team that already exists. You can invite them directly from the Teams list or from within Team Settings — use whichever is quicker for you.

<Info>
  Only **Owners** or **Admins** can invite new members. When you invite someone, you assign them a role: **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Member**.
</Info>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From the Teams list">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
        Access your account dashboard.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to Teams">
        Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the invitation menu">
        Locate the team, click on the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Invite team member**.

        <Frame>
          ![Invite team member option from the Teams list](/images/teams/invite-members-1.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter details">
        Input the email address of the new member and select their role (**Admin** or **Member**).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the invite">
        Click **Send invite**.

        <Frame>
          ![Entering member email and role](/images/teams/invite-members-2.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From team settings">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
        Access your account dashboard.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to Teams">
        Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open the edit page">
        Go to the **Teams** page, click the **three horizontal dots** for the team, and select **Edit team**.

        <Frame>
          ![Edit team option](/images/teams/invite-members-3.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Go to the Members tab">
        Click on the **Members** tab at the top of the page.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add a member">
        Click the blue **\+ Add team member** button in the top right.

        <Frame>
          ![Add team member button in the Members tab](/images/teams/invite-members-4.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Enter details">
        Enter the email address and select the role.

        <Frame>
          ![Entering member details](/images/teams/invite-members-5.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the invite">
        Click **Send invitation**.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
