---

[**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home) allows you to break your regular schedule day into multiple slots, allowing you to easily take time out of the calendar for consistent events such as lunch breaks, coffee breaks, or even recreational breaks. It lets you open specific slots for specific days. These slots can be copied to other days as well, or you can open different slots on different days. It's your Cal ID, your choice.
