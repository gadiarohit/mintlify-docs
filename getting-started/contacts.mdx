The [**Contacts**](https://cal.id/contacts) section is your built-in address book in Cal ID. It automatically saves everyone who books time with you, so you can search, edit, and schedule with them from one place.

## How contacts are created

Contacts land in your address book two ways:

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Automatic** | Every time someone schedules via your Cal ID URL, the system creates a contact with their **Name, Email, and Phone Number**. |
| **Manual** | Click the **"+ Add Contact"** button at the top right to add someone yourself. |

<Frame>
  ![Add Contact button at the top right of the Contacts page](/images/getting-started/contacts-1.png)
</Frame>

### Add a contact manually

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Add Contact form">
    Click the **"+ Add Contact"** button at the top right of the Contacts page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their details">
    In the pop-up, fill in their **Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and private Notes**, then save.

    <Frame>
      ![Add contact pop-up form](/images/getting-started/contacts-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Finding and viewing contacts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for a person">
    Type a **Name, Email, or Phone Number** into the search bar at the top left to quickly find anyone.

    <Frame>
      ![Contacts search bar](/images/getting-started/contacts-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their full profile">
    Click the **three dots (...)** under the **Actions** column and select **View Profile**.

    <Frame>
      ![Actions menu with View Profile option](/images/getting-started/contacts-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review their history">
    The profile shows their contact info, your private notes, and a clear list of all **Upcoming Meetings** and **Meeting History** (Past/Cancelled).

    <Frame>
      ![Contact profile with meeting history](/images/getting-started/contacts-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Edit a contact

To update someone's details, click **Edit Contact**. A pop-up opens where you can change the contact information. Click **Update Contact** to save your changes.

<Frame>
  ![Edit contact pop-up](/images/getting-started/contacts-6.png)
</Frame>

## Taking action with contacts

Click the **three dots (...)** under the **Actions** column to perform several quick tasks:

### A. Share availability

If a contact needs to book a new time with you, click **Share Availability**.

<Frame>
  ![Share Availability option in the Actions menu](/images/getting-started/contacts-7.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the event type">
    Select the **Event Type** you want them to book.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting an event type to share](/images/getting-started/contacts-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it their way">
    **Copy the Link**, send it directly via **WhatsApp**, or open a **Gmail** draft with the link included.

    <Frame>
      ![Copy link, WhatsApp, and Gmail sharing options](/images/getting-started/contacts-9.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### B. Direct scheduling

You can book a meeting for a contact directly, without sending a link:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Schedule Meeting">
    Click **Schedule Meeting** from the Actions menu.

    <Frame>
      ![Schedule Meeting option in the Actions menu](/images/getting-started/contacts-10.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the event type">
    Choose the **Event Type**.

    <Frame>
      ![Choosing an event type for direct scheduling](/images/getting-started/contacts-11.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the date and time slot">
    Select the **Date and Time Slot**.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting a date and time slot](/images/getting-started/contacts-12.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in booking questions">
    Fill in any additional booking questions. The Name and Email are filled automatically from their contact info.

    <Frame>
      ![Booking questions pre-filled with contact info](/images/getting-started/contacts-13.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm booking">
    Click **Confirm Booking**. The meeting will now appear in your **Bookings** tab and **Unified Calendar**.

    <Frame>
      ![Confirmed booking shown in the Bookings tab](/images/getting-started/contacts-14.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Direct scheduling is ideal when you're on a call and want to lock in a follow-up on the spot — no back-and-forth link needed.
</Tip>

## Deleting a contact

If you no longer need a contact in your list:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the profile">
    Go to **View Profile** for that contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete the contact">
    Click the red **Delete Contact** button on the left side of their profile page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Delete Contact button on the profile page](/images/getting-started/contacts-15.png)
</Frame>
