[Cal ID](https://cal.id/home) integration with Razorpay allows you to accept payments while users book your events. When someone selects your event, they must pay the amount to confirm the booking. Once the payment is successful, the meeting is automatically scheduled. You can also set refund rules and payment amounts for each event type.

**Cal ID uses Razorpay to allow you to receive payments when someone books your paid event type.**

To start taking payments, please follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search for Razorpay">
    Go to the [Cal ID](https://cal.id/home) App store and search for **Razorpay**.

    <Frame>
      ![Razorpay in the Cal ID App store](/images/apps/razorpay-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install and connect your account">
    Click **Install**, then connect the Razorpay account with your credentials.

    <Frame>
      ![Razorpay login screen](/images/apps/razorpay-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize the connection">
    Once logged in, click on the **Authorize** button to connect your account with Cal ID.

    <Frame>
      ![Razorpay authorization screen](/images/apps/razorpay-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the installation">
    Once authorized, you will be redirected to the Cal ID page, where you can see that the Razorpay app has been installed. Now you can add the Razorpay app as a payment method to any of your event types, whether it is a new or an existing one.

    <Frame>
      ![Razorpay app installed in Cal ID](/images/apps/razorpay-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Add Razorpay to an Event Type?

1. Go to **Event Types**.
2. Select the event type you want to add Razorpay to, and click **Edit**.
3. Go to the **Apps** section.
4. Turn **ON** the Razorpay app toggle.
5. Enter the amount you want to collect for this event type.
6. Save it.

Cal ID also provides a **Refund Policy** option. You can choose what happens if a user cancels a paid booking:

- **Always** – The user will always get a refund.
- **Never** – The user will not get a refund.
- **If cancelled \{defined\} business/calendar days before** – The user will get a refund only if they cancel at least \{defined\} business/calendar days before the event.

<Frame>
  ![Razorpay refund policy options](/images/apps/razorpay-5.png)
</Frame>

Now your booking page looks like this:

<Frame>
  ![Booking page with Razorpay payment](/images/apps/razorpay-6.png)

Once the user fills in the required questions, they need to click the **Pay to Book** button and complete the payment. After the payment is successful, the meeting will be scheduled.

That's it. You will receive payments as soon as someone books you using this event type.
