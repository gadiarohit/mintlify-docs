This document provides an updated overview of how to interpret analytics for Routing Forms in Cal ID. These insights allow you to track the effectiveness of your qualifying questions, visualize user drop-off points, and optimize your lead conversion funnel.

## Accessing Updated Routing Insights

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your main account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Insights">
    Click on the **Insights** in the left-hand sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Routing Tab">
    Click on the **Routing** tab located at the top left of the insights page to view analytics specific to your forms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Routing Metrics & Definitions

### Routing Form Summary

This scorecard provides a high-level view of form performance within your selected date range:

- **Total Response**: How many visitors completed and submitted the form.
- **Successful Routes**: The number of visitors who met your criteria and were successfully redirected to a destination (booking page).
- **UnSuccessful Routes**: The number of visitors who met your criteria and were successfully redirected to a destination but did not complete the booking.

### Routing Funnel Analysis

It shows date-wise trends for Total submissions, Successful Routings, and Accepted Bookings.

## Performance Graphs & Data Trends

- **Submission Timeline**: A graph showing daily or weekly form submissions, allowing you to correlate form activity with marketing campaigns.
- **Drop-off Visualization**: A visual representation of where users stop completing the form, helping you identify confusing questions.

  ![Submission timeline and drop-off visualization](/images/insights/routing-performance-3.png)

## Filtering and Customizing Data

- **Date Range Selector**: Use the dropdown in the top right to analyze data from specific periods, such as "Last 30 Days" or "Custom Range".

<Frame>
  ![Date range selector](/images/insights/routing-performance-4.png)
</Frame>

- **Form Selector**: If you have multiple routing forms, use the dropdown to view insights for a **Specific Form** or **All Forms**.

<Frame>
  ![Form selector dropdown](/images/insights/routing-performance-5.png)
</Frame>

- **Account Filter**: Switch between **Personal** routing data or specific **Team** routing data using the filter in the top left.

<Frame>
  ![Account filter](/images/insights/routing-performance-6.png)
</Frame>

- **Export Data**: Click the **Download** button in the top right to export your routing data as a CSV or PDF for advanced analysis.

<Frame>
  ![Download export button](/images/insights/routing-performance-7.png)
</Frame>
