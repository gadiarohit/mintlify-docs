In **Cal ID**, the **Internal Team Meeting** feature lets you quickly find common free time among your colleagues and book a meeting without ever leaving your dashboard.

<Info>
  Unlike a public booking link where others book you, this feature is designed for you to proactively schedule a meeting with your own team members based on their real-time availability.
</Info>

## Schedule an internal team meeting

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Team Availability">
    Log in to your Cal ID account, navigate to the **Availability** page in the sidebar, and click the **Team Availability** tab.

    <Frame>
      ![Team Availability tab on the Availability page](/images/teams/internal-meeting-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select members">
    You'll see a list of your team members. Check the boxes next to the specific members you want to include in the meeting.

    <Frame>
      ![Selecting team members for the meeting](/images/teams/internal-meeting-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find common ground">
    After selecting the members, click the **Book Members** button.

    <Frame>
      ![Book Members button](/images/teams/internal-meeting-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="View available slots">
    A popup window appears showing a calendar. It **only** shows the time slots where **all** selected members are simultaneously free (common availability).

    <Frame>
      ![Calendar showing common availability slots](/images/teams/internal-meeting-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book the time">
    Select the date and specific time slot that works for everyone, fill out the booking questions (meeting title, description, etc.), and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next?

Once you click **Confirm**, the system handles the rest of the communication:

- **Calendar sync**: The meeting is automatically added to the personal calendars of every selected team member.
- **Email notifications**: All selected members immediately receive an email invitation with the meeting details, location link, and time.
- **Centralized record**: The meeting also appears in your **Bookings** tab within Cal ID for easy management.

<Tip>
  **Quick syncs:** This is the fastest way to schedule emergency meetings or internal reviews. Instead of messaging everyone asking "Are you free at 2 PM?", the **Team Availability** tab gives you the answer instantly.
</Tip>
