If you want to customize how your event types appear on your public booking page, you can easily rearrange them. This allows you to prioritize your most important meetings so attendees see them first.

## How to rearrange your event types

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    Click on the **Event Types** tab in the left-hand sidebar to see your full list of active and inactive events.

    <Frame>
      ![Event Types list](/images/event-types/reorder-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Reorder Icon">
    Hover your mouse cursor over the event type you wish to move. A **6-dot icon (⣿)** will appear on the far left side of that specific event card.

    <Frame>
      ![Reorder 6-dot icon](/images/event-types/reorder-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag and Drop">
    - Click and hold the **6-dot icon**.
    - Drag the event type up or down to your preferred position in the list.
    - Release the mouse button to drop it into place.

    <Frame>
      ![Dragging an event type](/images/event-types/reorder-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Instant Sync">
    Once you move an event type in your dashboard, the new order is automatically saved and will be instantly reflected on your **Public Booking Page**.

    <Frame>
      ![Updated order on public booking page](/images/event-types/reorder-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why change the order?

- **Prioritize Popular Meetings**: Place your most frequently booked sessions (like "Intro Calls" or "Demos") at the very top so users don't have to scroll.
- **Logical Grouping**: Organise your meetings by duration (e.g., all 15-minute calls followed by 60-minute calls) or by category to make it easier for attendees to choose.
- **Highlight Seasonal Events**: If you have a temporary or high-priority event type, you can move it to the top position to ensure it gets maximum visibility.
