This document explains how to set your preferred language, adjust your primary timezone, and manage travel schedules to keep your bookings accurate while you are on the move.

## Steps to access general settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard at [Cal ID](https://cal.id/home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click on your profile name and select **Settings** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the General page">
    Click on **General** in the left-hand sidebar to view your basic account configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting your preferences

### Language

- **What it does**: This changes the language of the words you see while using your Cal ID dashboard.
- **How to change**: Simply select your preferred language from the **Language** dropdown list.

<Frame>
  ![Language dropdown](/images/settings/general-1.png)
</Frame>

### Timezone

- **What it does**: This sets your main home timezone so that all your meeting times are calculated correctly.
- **How to change**: Select your local city or region from the **Timezone** dropdown menu.

<Frame>
  ![Timezone dropdown](/images/settings/general-2.png)
</Frame>

### Travel schedule

**What it does**: This feature allows you to plan your trip ahead of time to keep your existing schedule in a different timezone and prevent clients from accidentally booking you at midnight while you are traveling.

<Frame>
  ![Travel schedule section](/images/settings/general-3.png)
</Frame>

**How to set it up:**

- Click the **Add** button next to **Travel Schedule**.

<Frame>
  ![Add travel schedule button](/images/settings/general-4.png)
</Frame>

- **Time range**: Select the dates you will be traveling using the calendar picker.

<Frame>
  ![Travel schedule date picker](/images/settings/general-5.png)
</Frame>

- **No end date**: If you don't know when you are coming back, you can toggle the **Schedule timezone without end date** switch to **ON**.

<Frame>
  ![No end date toggle](/images/settings/general-6.png)
</Frame>

- **Timezone**: Select the timezone of the place you are visiting.

<Frame>
  ![Travel timezone selector](/images/settings/general-7.png)
</Frame>

- **Update**: Click the blue **Update** button to update your changes and activate the travel schedule.
- Now your settings look like this:

<Frame>
  ![Updated travel schedule settings](/images/settings/general-8.png)
</Frame>
