### Adding Zoom as the meeting location for the event type

To install Zoom and add it as the meeting location for your event type, you need to follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Cal ID App store">
    First, you need to visit the [**Cal ID App store**](https://cal.id/apps).

    <Frame>
      ![Cal ID App store](/images/apps/zoom-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Zoom">
    Search for Zoom and click on **Details**.

    <Frame>
      ![Zoom search result in the App store](/images/apps/zoom-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Once the Cal ID Zoom app page opens up, click on **Install App**.

    <Frame>
      ![Zoom app install page](/images/apps/zoom-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Zoom">
    Once you click on Install, you'll be redirected to your Zoom account (sign in if you haven't already) and allow the necessary permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the installation">
    Now, you should be redirected to Cal ID's Installed Apps page with the Conferencing tab selected, and you should see Zoom Video in the list. Zoom Video is now successfully installed in your Cal ID account.

    <Frame>
      ![Zoom Video in the Installed Apps list](/images/apps/zoom-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Zoom as your default (optional)">
    If you want to make any video conferencing app your default, click on the three dots (on the right side) of that app and set it as default. Once you do this, every new event you create in the future will automatically use this video conferencing app, unless you change it again later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Zoom as the event location">
    Now, you can head to your event type settings page and add a location following this guide. Once you have added Zoom Video as your event-type location, you will see it as shown in the image below. Click on **Save** and all your meetings should now have Zoom video as the default location.

    <Frame>
      ![Zoom Video set as the event type location](/images/apps/zoom-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it!
