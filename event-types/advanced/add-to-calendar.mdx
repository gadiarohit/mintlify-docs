In **Cal ID**, the **Add Events to Calendar** feature (often found under the "Advanced" tab or general settings) gives you total control over which of your connected calendars should store a specific event's bookings.

## Selecting where events are created

By default, Cal ID adds new bookings to your "Primary" calendar. However, this feature allows you to override that default for specific event types, helping you keep your professional and personal lives perfectly separated.

## Why use "Add Events to Calendar"?

- **Organization:** If you have separate calendars for "Work," "Side Projects," and "Personal," you can ensure each booking lands exactly where it belongs.
- **Team Management:** In a team environment, you can direct specific event types to a shared team calendar rather than your private one.
- **Visibility:** It helps you see your availability at a glance by ensuring your most relevant calendar is the one being populated with new meetings.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    Click on **Event Types** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Event">
    Click the specific event you want to customize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced">
    Click on the **Advanced** tab in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate Add Events to Calendar">
    Look for the dropdown menu labeled **Add events to calendar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Your Calendar">
    Select the specific connected calendar (e.g., Google, Outlook, or Apple) where you want these bookings to appear.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** button at the top right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- **Automatic Creation:** Once a guest finishes booking, Cal ID immediately pushes the event details to the specific calendar you selected.
- **Conflict Checking:** Even if you choose a specific calendar to **add** events to, Cal ID will still check **all** your connected calendars for conflicts to ensure you don't get double-booked.
- **Independence:** Changing this setting for one event type (like "Project Kickoff") will not affect where your other event types (like "Quick Chat") are stored.

<Tip>
  To set a global default for **all** event types, head to your **`/settings/calendars`**. From there, you can choose one primary calendar that will be used whenever an event-specific one isn't chosen.
</Tip>
