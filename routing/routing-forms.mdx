## Overview

Cal ID's Routing Forms helps people quickly book the right appointment with the right person.

It works by asking the person booking to answer a few questions. Based on their answers, the system automatically directs them to the correct person, a specific event, or an external website.

This saves time for everyone and avoids mistakes, like booking with the wrong person or choosing the wrong type of meeting.

## Step 1: Create a New Form

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Routing">
    Log in to your Cal ID account and click on **Routing** in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Creation">
    Click the **\+ New** button (or "Create Routing form") in the top right corner.

    <Frame>
      ![New routing form button](/images/routing/routing-forms-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Ownership">
    If you are part of a team, the system will ask if this form is for your **Personal** account or for a specific **Team**.

    <Frame>
      ![Choose personal or team ownership](/images/routing/routing-forms-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Form Details">
    An **Add new form** pop-up will appear:

    - **Title**: Give your form a clear name (e.g., "New Client Inquiry").
    - **Description**: Briefly explain the purpose of the form for your internal reference.

    <Frame>
      ![Add new form pop-up with title and description](/images/routing/routing-forms-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create">
    Click the **Create** button to proceed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Design the Form (Questions)

You will be redirected to the **Form** tab to build your questionnaire.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Questions">
    Click the **Add question** button to create your first field. You can add as many as you want.

    <Frame>
      ![Add question button](/images/routing/routing-forms-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Each Question">
    - **Label**: The question the user will see (e.g., "What is your budget?").
    - **Identifier**: A unique internal name used for setting up routing rules later.
    - **Type**: Choose the format of the answer: **Short Text**, **Long Text**, **Single Select**, **Multi-select**, **Email**, **Phone**, or **Number**.
    - **Required**: Select **Yes** if the user must answer this to proceed.

    <Frame>
      ![Question configuration fields](/images/routing/routing-forms-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    After adding all your questions, click the **Save** button in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your form">
    After adding the required questions, your page will look like this:

    <Frame>
      ![Completed form with questions](/images/routing/routing-forms-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 3: Set Up Routing Rules

Navigate to the **Routing** tab to decide where users go based on their answers.

If you don't want to add any route, then set the Fallback route, where you want to send the booker after filling out the required questions.

<Frame>
  ![Routing tab with fallback route](/images/routing/routing-forms-7.png)
</Frame>

To add a route:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a new Route">
    Click **\+ Add a new Route** to add a route.

    <Frame>
      ![Add a new route button](/images/routing/routing-forms-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Rules">
    Click **\+ Add rule** to link a question to a value (e.g., "If Budget is Greater than 5000").

    <Frame>
      ![Add rule to a route](/images/routing/routing-forms-9.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define Logic">
    If you add more than one Rule then you have to define logic. Next to "For responses matching the following criteria," choose a logic filter:

    - **All**: The user must meet **every** rule in this route to be redirected.
    - **Any**: The user only needs to meet **one** of the rules.
    - **None**: The route applies if the user meets **none** of the rules.

    <Frame>
      ![Logic filter options](/images/routing/routing-forms-10.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Destination (Send Booker To)">
    - **Custom Page**: Display a specific "Thank You" or "Waitlist" message.

    <Frame>
      ![Custom page destination](/images/routing/routing-forms-11.png)
    </Frame>

    - **External Redirect**: Send the user to another website (e.g., your homepage).

    <Frame>
      ![External redirect destination](/images/routing/routing-forms-12.png)
    </Frame>

    - **Event Redirect**: Send the user directly to a specific booking page (e.g., a "Career Counselling" event).

    <Frame>
      ![Event redirect destination](/images/routing/routing-forms-13.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fallback Route">
    At the bottom, set a **Fallback route** for users who do not match any of your specific rules.

    <Frame>
      ![Fallback route setting](/images/routing/routing-forms-14.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you want to embed the routing form on your website or page, then follow Step 4.

## Step 4: Embed the Form on Your Website

Once your logic is set, go to the **Embed** tab and choose the type.

<Frame>
  ![Embed tab with type options](/images/routing/routing-forms-15.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure Settings">
    Adjust the display settings for your form (such as theme, width, or Brand Color) if needed.

    <Frame>
      ![Embed display settings](/images/routing/routing-forms-16.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy Code">
    Click the **Copy Code** button. A pop-up will appear showing the snippet.

    <Frame>
      ![Copy code pop-up](/images/routing/routing-forms-17.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Paste this code into your website's HTML where you want the form to appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>
