This document explains how to set or change your account password, depending on how you originally created your Cal ID account.

## Steps to access password settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard at [Cal ID](https://cal.id/home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Open **Settings** from the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Password">
    Click on **Password** in the left-hand menu.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Scenarios for managing your password

### Scenario 1: You Signed Up Using "Sign in with Google"

If you created your account by clicking **Sign in with Google** (Gmail), you have not set a specific password for Cal ID yet.

- **What you will see**: On the password page, you will see a message stating: **"Your account is managed by Google"**.
- **How to create a password**:

1. Click the button labeled **"Create account password"**.
2. An email will be sent to your primary email address.
3. Open that email and click the link inside to set your new password.

<Frame>
  ![Account managed by Google message](/images/settings/password-1.png)
</Frame>

### Scenario 2: You Set a Password Previously

If you signed up with an email and password, or have previously added a password, you can change it here.

- **What you will see**: You will see fields for **Old password** and **New password**.
- **Steps to change**:

1. Enter your current password in the **Old password** field.
2. Enter your desired new password in the **New password** field.
3. Click **Update**.
