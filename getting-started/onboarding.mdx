Create your Cal ID account in a couple of minutes, then set up your profile, calendar, and video apps so you're ready to take bookings.

## Sign up for Cal ID

To get started, visit [https://cal.id/signup](https://cal.id/signup) and choose how you'd like to create your account.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Google">
    Signing up with Google is the fastest way to get started — it verifies your identity automatically.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Select Google">
        On the sign-up page, click the **Continue with Google** button.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Choose account">
        Select your preferred Google account from the pop-up.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Automatic verification">
        Your email is verified instantly. You'll be redirected straight to the [onboarding steps](#the-onboarding-process).
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Tip>
      Google sign-up skips the email verification step entirely, so you can jump straight into setting up your account.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email">
    Use this if you prefer a specific email address and a unique password.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Enter your email ID">
        Enter your **Email ID**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Create a password">
        Create a **Password** that meets the security requirements.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Register">
        Click the **Create Account** button.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Frame>
      ![Sign up form with email and password](/images/getting-started/onboarding-1.png)
    </Frame>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your email">
        Go to your inbox (or Spam folder) and look for the verification email from Cal ID.

        <Frame>
          ![Verification email from Cal ID](/images/getting-started/onboarding-2.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify your address">
        Click the link inside the email. Once verified, you'll be redirected to the [onboarding steps](#the-onboarding-process).

        <Frame>
          ![Email verified confirmation screen](/images/getting-started/onboarding-3.png)
        </Frame>
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## The onboarding process

Once verified, you'll be redirected to complete these three essential onboarding steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Profile details">
    Set up your basic profile information:

    - **Username:** Choose your unique Cal ID link (e.g., `cal.id/yourname`).
    - **Full Name:** Enter the name you want people to see when booking with you.
    - **Business Type:** Select your role from the dropdown menu.
    - **Timezone:** Select your local timezone to ensure meeting times are accurate.

    Click **Next Step**.

    <Frame>
      ![Profile details onboarding step](/images/getting-started/onboarding-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    Connect your preferred calendar so Cal ID can automatically check your availability:

    - Choose from options like **Google Calendar**, **Outlook**, **Zoho**, **Apple Calendar**, or **Microsoft Exchange**.
    - Click **Connect** next to your preferred service.

    <Note>
      You can also choose _I'll connect my calendar later_ at the bottom if you prefer.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
      ![Connect your calendar onboarding step](/images/getting-started/onboarding-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect video apps">
    Connect the apps you use for virtual meetings:

    - Select from tools like **Google Meet**, **Zoom Video**, **Microsoft Teams**, **Facetime**, **Discord**, or **Riverside**.
    - Click **Connect** to link your accounts.

    Click **Finish**.

    <Frame>
      ![Connect video apps onboarding step](/images/getting-started/onboarding-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

After clicking Finish, you're welcomed to the [Cal ID Home Page](https://cal.id/home).

<Frame>
  ![Cal ID Home page after onboarding](/images/getting-started/onboarding-7.png)
</Frame>

<Check>
  You're all set — your Cal ID account is ready to take bookings.
</Check>
