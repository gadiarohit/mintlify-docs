Facilitate bookings without exposing your personal or business email. In [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home), the **Hide organizer's email** feature is a privacy-focused setting that keeps your primary contact information secure while still sending calendar invites and meeting details to attendees.

## Why use "Hide organizer's email"?

- **Maintain privacy:** Ideal for public figures, high-level executives, or anyone using a personal email address for business who doesn't want their inbox address widely distributed.
- **Prevent unsolicited follow-ups:** Hiding your email encourages guests to use official channels (like your website or support desk) for follow-up questions rather than emailing you directly.
- **Brand consistency:** For organizations, this keeps the focus on the "Event" or the "Team" rather than an individual person's email address.
- **Reduce spam:** Keeping your email off calendar invites prevents it from being scraped or added to marketing lists by external parties.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Log in to your [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home) account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    Go to the **Event Types** tab in the left sidebar and select the specific event you wish to modify.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced">
    Click on the **Advanced** tab in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the setting">
    Scroll down to the privacy section and locate **Hide organizer's email**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable">
    Switch the toggle to **ON**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the blue **Save** button at the top right to finalize the change.

    <Frame>
      ![Hide organizer's email toggle](/images/event-types/hide-organizer-email-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works for the booker

- **The calendar invite:** On the guest's calendar (Google, Outlook, etc.), the "Organizer" field typically shows your **Display Name** only, or a generic "no-reply" system address, rather than your actual email address.
- **The confirmation email:** The "From" address on the automated confirmation email is masked or replaced by Cal ID's system email.
- **Booking details:** Your email address is omitted from the booking summary page the guest sees after they finish scheduling.

<Info>
  This feature hides your email on the invite and confirmation page only. If you reply to an automated notification or send a manual email to the guest later, your email address will be visible — the setting is strictly for the **initial booking and automated calendar artifacts**. Also make sure your **Display Name** is set correctly in your account settings, since that is the primary way guests will identify you.
</Info>
