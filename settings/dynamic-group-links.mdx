In **Cal ID**, **Dynamic Group Links** are a revolutionary way to schedule meetings with multiple people on the fly without ever having to create a formal "Team" or a pre-defined event type.

## Using Dynamic Group Links

Traditional group scheduling requires you to set up a "Collective Event" in your dashboard first. Dynamic Group Links bypass this entirely by allowing you to create a collective booking link just by editing a URL.

### How it Works (The "\+" Method)

To create a group link instantly, simply take two or more Cal ID usernames and join them with a **plus (\+) sign** in the browser's address bar.

- **1-on-1 Link:** `cal.id/yourname`
- **Dynamic Group Link:** `cal.id/yourname+colleague`
- **Triple Group Link:** `cal.id/yourname+colleague+manager`

The system will automatically find the times where **all** participants are available and only show those slots to the booker.

## Why use Dynamic Group Links?

- **No Setup Required:** You don't need to log in to the dashboard or create a new event type every time you want to loop someone in.
- **Perfect for Ad-hoc Meetings:** Ideal for sales calls where you need to bring in an engineer, or a doctor's appointment that needs an assistant present.
- **Global Coordination:** It automatically handles the cross-referencing of everyone's timezones and busy slots in seconds.

## Privacy & Control

This is an **opt-in** feature to ensure you aren't added to random group meetings without your consent.

1. **To Enable/Disable:** Head to **Settings \> General**
2. **Toggle the Switch:** Look for the **Dynamic Group Links** toggle.

- **ON:** Others can add your username to a dynamic link to include you in a booking.
- **OFF:** Your username will be blocked from dynamic links, ensuring people can only book you via your established event types.
