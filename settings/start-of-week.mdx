In **Cal ID**, the **Start of week** feature allows you to choose which day of the week appears first on your calendar. Changing this setting reorganizes the layout of your **public booking page** to match your preferred work schedule.

## Why Change the Start of the Week?

The day you select becomes the "anchor" for your calendar grid.

- **Standard Business View**: Many professionals prefer a **Monday** start so that the work week (Monday–Friday) is grouped together.
- **Traditional View**: Selecting **Sunday** places the weekend at the start of the calendar grid.
- **Visual Only**: This change only moves the columns; it does **not** change your available hours or existing meetings.

## Complete Step-by-Step Instructions

Follow these steps to update your calendar layout:

| **Step** | **Action** | **Detail** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Go to Settings** | Locate the sidebar on the left side of your dashboard and click on **Settings**. |
| **2** | **Open General Tab** | Inside the settings menu, click on the **General** sub-tab. |
| **3** | **Find "Start of week"** | Scroll down the page until you see the dropdown menu labeled **Start of week**. |
| **4** | **Choose Your Day** | Click the menu and select your preferred day (for example, **Monday**). |
| **5** | **Save the Change** | Click the blue **Update** button at the bottom of the screen to save your new layout. |

## What Your Guests Will See

Once you click **Update**, your public booking page will immediately reflect the new layout.

- **New Column Order**: If you selected **Monday**, the first column on the far left will be **MON**, followed by **TUE**, and ending with **SUN** on the far right.
- **Correct Dates**: All your dates automatically shift to stay under the correct day (e.g., if February 5th is a Thursday, it will stay under the **THU** column regardless of which day the week starts).
- **Easy Navigation**: This makes it much easier for your clients to view your availability based on the work week they are used to.

<Tip>
  Most international businesses use a **Monday** start to keep the work week clear for clients. If you primarily work with clients in North America, a **Sunday** start is often the most familiar choice.
</Tip>
