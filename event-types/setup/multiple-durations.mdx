In **Cal ID**, you don't have to create multiple separate events just to offer different meeting lengths. You can use **Multiple Durations** to let your guests decide how much time they need. Bookers can select the duration of the booking.

## Offering multiple durations

This feature allows a single event type to have multiple time options (for example: 15, 20, and 60 minutes). The guest picks the length that suits them best before choosing a time slot.

### Why use multiple durations?

- **Cleaner Profile:** Keep your booking page simple by grouping different time options under one event.
- **Guest Flexibility:** It gives the booker the power to choose exactly how much of your time they need.

## How to set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    Click on **Event Types** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Event">
    Click on the event you want to edit (e.g., "Interview").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Multiple Durations">
    Scroll down to the **Duration** section. Switch the toggle for **"Allow booker to select duration"** to **ON** (it will turn blue).

    <Frame>
      ![Allow booker to select duration toggle](/images/event-types/multiple-durations-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your Options">
    In the **Available durations** field, select or type the different lengths of time you want to offer (e.g., 5 mins, 15 mins, 20 mins, 60 mins).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Default">
    Choose a **Default duration** from the dropdown menu. This is the time length that will be pre-selected for the guest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the **Save** button at the top of the page.

    <Frame>
      ![Available durations and default duration](/images/event-types/multiple-durations-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it looks for the booker

- When a guest visits your booking link, they will see your list of available durations.
- Once they select a duration, the calendar will automatically update to show only the time slots that can fit that specific meeting length.

<Frame>
  ![Duration options on the booking page](/images/event-types/multiple-durations-3.png)
</Frame>

## Important things to know

- **Smart Availability:** If a guest picks a 60-minute meeting, Cal ID will automatically hide any smaller gaps in your schedule that aren't long enough for a full hour.
- **Easy Management:** You can quickly add or remove duration options at any time by clicking the "x" next to a time in the **Available durations** list.
