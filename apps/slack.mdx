Stay on top of your schedule by getting instant alerts where you already work. By connecting **Cal ID** to **Slack** via **Zapier**, **Pabbly**, **Make** or **ViaSocket** you can stop checking your email and get notified the moment a booking happens.

### Why use this?

- **Instant Alerts:** Know immediately when a new client books you.
- **Team Sync:** Send notifications to a shared channel so your team stays updated.
- **All-in-One Place:** Track new bookings, reschedules, and cancellations without leaving Slack.

## Step 1: Install Zapier (any automation tool) in Cal ID

1. Log in to your **Cal ID** account.
2. Go to the **App Store** in the sidebar.
3. Find the **Zapier** app and click **Install**.
4. **Generate** the API key and **Copy** it.

## Step 2: Create Your Automation (The Zap)

Log in to your [Zapier account](https://zapier.com) and follow these 3 mini-steps:

### A. The Trigger (When this happens...)

- **App:** Choose **Cal ID**.
- **Event:** Select **Booking Created**.
- **Account:** Connect your Cal ID account (you may be asked for your API Key).

### B. The Action (Do this...)

- **App:** Choose **Slack**.
- **Event:** Select **Send Channel Message** (to alert a team) or **Send Direct Message** (to alert only yourself).
- **Message:** Type what you want the alert to say. You can click the "Insert Data" button to include the **Booker's Name** and **Meeting Time**.

### C. The Finish

- Click **Test** to see a sample message appear in Slack.
- If it looks good, click **Publish**. **That's it!**

## Other Useful Triggers

You can create separate **Zaps** for these other common events:

- **Booking Rescheduled:** Get an alert if a client moves the meeting.
- **Booking Cancelled:** Know instantly if a slot has opened back up.
- **Meeting Ended:** Get a reminder in Slack to send a follow-up email right after the call.
