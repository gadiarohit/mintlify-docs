**Date Overrides** are for when your schedule needs to be different for just one or two days. They allow you to change your hours for a specific date without changing your regular routine. With availability date overrides, you can pick any date that you're currently available and set specific hours for availability on that day or **mark yourself entirely unavailable**. Taking a half day next Thursday and Friday to do some last-minute shopping? Simply set an override on those specific days to block yourself off.

## When to use this

- **Taking a day off:** If you are going on a holiday.
- **A one-time appointment:** If you need to be busy for 2 hours on a specific day.
- **Special hours:** If you want to work on a holiday just for a few hours.

## How to add an override

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Availability">
    Click on the schedule you want to adjust.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Overrides">
    Scroll down to the bottom to the **Date Overrides** section.

    <Frame>
      ![Date Overrides section of a schedule](/images/availability/date-overrides-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add an override and pick the date">
    Click **"\+ Add an override"** and pick the date. Select the specific day on the calendar you want to change.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Time">
    - **To be busy all day:** Toggle ON the Mark unavailable (All day) field.
    - **To change the time:** Select which hours you are free for that specific day only.

    <Frame>
      ![Setting override hours or marking unavailable](/images/availability/date-overrides-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Save Overrides** and then click the main **Save** button at the top of the page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- **Overrides take priority:** If your regular schedule says you are free, but your Override says you are busy, Cal ID will show you as **Busy**.
- **Temporary:** Once the date has passed, your calendar goes back to its normal weekly schedule automatically.
