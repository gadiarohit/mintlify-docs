This document explains how to customize the visual theme and button visibility for your team's public booking page.

## Steps to customize team appearance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Teams">
    Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Team Menu">
    Locate the team you wish to edit, click the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Edit team**.

    <Frame>
      ![Edit team menu](/images/appearance/team-appearance-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Appearance">
    Click on the **Appearance** tab at the top of the settings page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Visual Settings">
    Configure the options described below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Booking page theme

Select the theme your clients will see when visiting your public page: **System default**, **Light**, or **Dark**.

The theme selection determines the background and text colors your client interacts with when they view your team's public page. Choosing a specific theme ensures a consistent experience regardless of the user's personal browser settings.

<Frame>
  ![Team booking page theme](/images/appearance/team-appearance-2.png)
</Frame>

### Hide book a team member button

Toggle this to **ON** to remove the shortcut button from your public page.

<Frame>
  ![Hide Book a Team Member toggle](/images/appearance/team-appearance-3.png)
</Frame>

By default, when a user opens your team's public booking page, they will see a button labeled **Book a team member instead** at the very bottom of the page, following the list of available event types.

- **When Toggle is OFF (Default)**: The button is visible, allowing guests to bypass team events and book with individuals directly.

<Frame>
  ![Book a team member button visible](/images/appearance/team-appearance-4.png)
</Frame>

- **When Toggle is ON**: The button is hidden. This is useful if you want to ensure all bookings follow your team's specific **Round Robin** or **Collective** logic rather than letting guests pick a specific person manually.

<Frame>
  ![Book a team member button hidden](/images/appearance/team-appearance-5.png)
</Frame>

### Hide team profile link

Toggle this to **ON** to hide the link to your team's profile on all booking pages.

<Frame>
  ![Hide team profile link toggle](/images/appearance/team-appearance-6.png)
</Frame>
