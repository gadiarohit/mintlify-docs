The **Custom Branding** section lets you personalize your booking experience and align it with your business identity—a banner, social links, brand colors, and your own logo and favicon.

<Info>
  These settings apply to your **personal** public booking page. To brand a team's page instead, see [Custom Branding For Teams](/branding/teams).
</Info>

## Open Custom Branding

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Custom Branding">
    Select the **Custom Branding** tab, then configure the features below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Public page banner

Upload a professional header image with exact **3200 x 320 px** dimensions. **For more detail, visit:** [**Public Page Banner**](/branding/banner)

  ![Public page banner setting](/images/branding/personal-1.png)
</Frame>

## Social profiles

Enable and link your **LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube,** and other professional profiles. **For more detail, visit:** [**Social Profiles**](/branding/social-profiles)

<Frame>
  ![Social profiles setting](/images/branding/personal-2.png)
</Frame>

## Custom brand colors

Set specific **Hex codes** for both light and dark themes to match your brand palette. **For more detail, visit:** [**Custom Brand Colors**](/branding/brand-colors)

<Frame>
  ![Custom brand colors setting](/images/branding/personal-3.png)
</Frame>

## Custom brand logo and favicon

Upload your **600 x 400 px** logo and browser tab icon to fully customize your page. **For more detail, visit:** [**Custom Brand Logo and Favicon**](/branding/logo-favicon)

  ![Custom brand logo and favicon setting](/images/branding/personal-4.png)
