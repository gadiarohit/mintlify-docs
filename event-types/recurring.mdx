The **Recurring** feature allows you to create a series of meetings based on a schedule, enabling attendees to subscribe to a recurring series rather than just booking a single occurrence.

## How to enable recurring events

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Event Types">
    From your Cal ID dashboard, click on [**Event Types**](https://cal.id/event-types) in the left-hand menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit Your Event">
    Find the event you want to make recurring and click the **Edit** (pencil) icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Recurring Tab">
    In the top settings bar, click on **Recurring**.

    <Frame>
      ![Recurring tab in the event editor](/images/event-types/recurring-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn it On">
    Switch the **Recurring Event** toggle to **ON**.

    <Frame>
      ![Recurring Event toggle switched on](/images/event-types/recurring-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Frequency">
    - **Repeats every**: Enter a number and choose the interval (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Yearly).
    - **Example: "Repeats every 1 week" means the meeting happens every seven days.**

    <Frame>
      ![Repeats every frequency setting](/images/event-types/recurring-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing the booking style

Cal ID gives you two ways to handle how many sessions an attendee books:

### Option A: allow attendee to choose (flexible)

Use this if you want the attendee to decide how many sessions they need within your limits.

- Choose: For what you want to set up

<Frame>
  ![Flexible occurrence option](/images/event-types/recurring-4.png)
</Frame>

- **Maximum occurrences**: The number of sessions available in the series.

<Frame>
  ![Maximum occurrences setting](/images/event-types/recurring-5.png)
</Frame>

- **Minimum occurrences**: The smallest number of sessions a user **must** book to complete the checkout.

<Frame>
  ![Minimum occurrences setting](/images/event-types/recurring-6.png)
</Frame>

- **Default occurrences**: The number of sessions that will be automatically selected when the attendee opens the page.

<Frame>
  ![Default occurrences setting](/images/event-types/recurring-7.png)
</Frame>

### Option B: fixed number of sessions (mandatory)

Use this if the series requires a set number of meetings (e.g., a "10-Session Masterclass").

- Select the **Fixed number of sessions** option.
- **Number of occurrences**: Enter the exact number of meetings the attendee is required to book.

<Frame>
  ![Fixed number of sessions setting](/images/event-types/recurring-8.png)
</Frame>

- On your public page, the attendee will see exactly how many times the event occurs (e.g., "Occurs 10 times").

<Frame>
  ![Public page showing fixed occurrence count](/images/event-types/recurring-9.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Public page recurring event view](/images/event-types/recurring-10.png)
</Frame>

## How it works for the attendee

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the First Slot">
    The attendee chooses their starting date and time.

    <Frame>
      ![Attendee picking the first slot](/images/event-types/recurring-11.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Quantity">
    If you enabled "User-Defined" occurrences, the attendee can use a \+, - symbol or enter the value to choose how many sessions they want.

    - They cannot go below your **Minimum** or above your **Maximum**.

    <Frame>
      ![Attendee selecting session quantity](/images/event-types/recurring-12.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Once they click "Confirm," all sessions are booked simultaneously.

    <Frame>
      ![Attendee confirming recurring booking](/images/event-types/recurring-13.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Recurring Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Repeats every** | Sets the time gap between meetings (e.g., every 2 weeks). |
| **Minimum** | Ensures the attendee books at least X number of sessions. |
| **Fixed Number** | Forces the attendee to book a specific, non-negotiable amount. |
| **Default** | Pre-fills the session count to make booking faster for the user. |
