The **Look Busy** feature allows you to control how much of your actual availability is visible to bookers on your public page. Instead of showing all open slots, you can configure it to display only a specific percentage of your available time, creating a sense of urgency or managing your meeting load.

## Steps to enable and configure "look busy"

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    From your main dashboard, click the [**Event Types**](https://cal.id/event-types) tab in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and Edit">
    Find the specific event type you want to modify and click on the three dots and choose **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Advanced Tab">
    In the top navigation menu of the event editor, click on the **Advanced** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle Look Busy">
    Scroll down to locate **Look Busy** and switch the toggle to the **ON** position.

    <Frame>
      ![Look Busy toggle](/images/event-types/look-busy-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Availability Limits">
    - **Percentage of Slots**: Use the dropdown to select the percentage of your total available slots to make visible (e.g., set to 50% to hide half of your available times).

    <Frame>
      ![Percentage of slots dropdown](/images/event-types/look-busy-2.png)
    </Frame>

    - **Time Horizon**: Define how far into the future bookers can see availability by entering a number in the **Days** field and selecting **calendar days** or **business days** from the dropdown.

    <Frame>
      ![Time horizon days field](/images/event-types/look-busy-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Your Changes">
    Click the **Save** button in the top right corner to apply these limits to your public booking page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use case

If you are available all day Monday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (8 hours total) but set **Look Busy** to **50%**, Cal ID will only show **4 hours** worth of slots to your bookers, making your calendar look more in-demand while reserving the rest of the time for yourself.
