This document explains how to apply custom branding to a specific team's booking page, ensuring a consistent look and feel for panel meetings or round-robin events.

In **Cal ID**, the **Custom Branding** section allows you to personalize your booking experience and align it with your business identity.

To set up your brand:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Teams">
    Click on the **Teams** in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Team Menu">
    Locate the team, click the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Edit team**.

    <Frame>
      ![Edit team menu](/images/branding/teams-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Custom Branding Tab">
    Click on the **Custom Branding** tab at the top of the page.

    <Frame>
      ![Custom Branding tab](/images/branding/teams-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Public Page Banner

Upload a professional header image with exact **3200 x 320 px** dimensions. **For more detail, visit:** [**Public Page Banner For Team**](/branding/banner-team)

<Frame>
  ![Team public page banner setting](/images/branding/teams-3.png)
</Frame>

## Custom Brand Colors

Set specific **Hex codes** for both light and dark themes to match your brand palette. **For more detail, visit:** [**Custom Brand Colors For Team**](/branding/brand-colors-team)

<Frame>
  ![Team custom brand colors setting](/images/branding/teams-4.png)

## Custom Brand Logo and Favicon

Upload your **600 x 400 px** logo and browser tab icon to fully customize your page. **For more detail, visit:** [**Custom Brand Logo and Favicon for Team**](/branding/logo-favicon-team)
