Creating a schedule is only half the battle—you also have to tell your specific events (like a "Discovery Call" or "Technical Demo") which schedule to follow.

## How to Link a Schedule to an Event

- **Select Your Event**: Go to the Event Types dashboard and click on the specific event you want to edit.
- **Access Settings**: Click on the Availability tab within that event's settings.
- **Choose Your Availability:** Under the availability dropdown, select the specific name of the schedule you created.
- **Save:** Click Save.
