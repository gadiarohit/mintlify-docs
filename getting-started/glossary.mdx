Here's a list of commonly used words in the [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home) platform:

## The people (roles)

| Term | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Host** | The person who owns the meeting and the video link. |
| **Co-Host** | A required team member who must also be free for the meeting. |
| **Scheduler** | The person (client) who picks the time and books the meeting. |
| **Guest** | Someone invited by the scheduler; they aren't required to attend. |

## The meeting (details)

| Term | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Event Type** | A template for a meeting (e.g., "30-Minute Intro"). |
| **Location** | Where the meeting happens (Zoom, Google Meet, or Office). |
| **Availability** | The specific hours you choose to be "open" for bookings. |
| **Buffer Time** | A "breather" or gap added between meetings to avoid rushes. |

## Technical & Branding

| Term | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Slug** | The name at the end of your link (e.g., cal.id/**john**). |
| **Embed** | Putting your booking page directly inside your own website. |
| **Integration** | Connecting Cal ID to other apps like Slack or Google Calendar and many more... |
| **Workflow** | An automated action, like sending a text reminder before a call. |
| **Branding** | Customizing the page with your own logo and colors. |
