The **Teams** feature in Cal ID is a powerful tool designed to streamline collaborative scheduling. By grouping users together, you can manage shared availability, distribute workloads evenly, and ensure that clients can always find a time to meet with your department.

## Benefits of Using Teams

- **Shared Availability**: Instantly find times when team members are free to avoid scheduling conflicts.
- **Efficient Workload Distribution**: Automatically distribute meetings among available team member.
- **Cohesive Brand Image**: Present a unified scheduling page for your entire team.

## Step-by-Step Team Creation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Teams Page">
    Go to the [Teams Page](https://cal.id/teams).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Team">
    Click on the **\+ Add a team** button to start the setup process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Team Details">
    - Enter the **Team name**; this name will be reflected in your public URL.
    - Set the **Team URL**.

    <Note>
      The person who creates the team automatically becomes the **Owner**.
    </Note>

    <Frame>
      ![Entering team name and URL](/images/teams/overview-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Team Members">
    - Now, enter the email address of the person you want to invite.
    - Select their role: **Owner**, **Admin**, or **Member**.
    - Click on **Send invite**.

    <Frame>
      ![Adding team members and selecting roles](/images/teams/overview-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Understand Roles">
    - **Owner/Admin**: Can change any settings for the team.
    - **Member**: Can only see applied settings but cannot modify them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize Team">
    Click **Continue** to move to event creation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create a Team Event Type

After creating the team, you need to define how they will be booked:

1. **Title & Duration**: Enter a clear title for the event type and select the duration.
2. **Scheduling Type**: Choose how the meeting should be booked based on your use case.
3. **Description**: Write a brief description about the event type for your clients.

<Frame>
  ![Creating a team event type](/images/teams/overview-3.png)
</Frame>

## Understanding Scheduling Types

- **Round Robin**
  **Use Case**: Evenly distributing sales demos among account executives.
  **Description**: Meeting is booked with one available team member automatically, reducing wait times for clients. For more details, visit [Round Robin Events](/teams/round-robin).
- **Collective**
  **Use Case**: Panel interviews or technical consultations requiring multiple team members.
  **Description**: Meeting is booked only when **all** selected team members are free simultaneously. For more details, visit [Collective Events](/teams/collective).
- **Managed**
  **Use Case**: Ensuring consistent event settings across the team.
  **Description**: Meetings are booked based on a template controlled strictly by the admin, ensuring standardized booking processes. For more details, visit [Managed Events](/teams/managed).
