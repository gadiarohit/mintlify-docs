Set your preferred language, adjust your primary timezone, and manage travel schedules so your bookings stay accurate even when you are on the move.

## Steps to access general settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard at [Cal ID](https://cal.id/home).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click your profile name and select **Settings** from the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the General page">
    Click **General** in the left-hand sidebar to view your basic account configuration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Setting your preferences

### Language

- **What it does** — changes the language of the words you see while using your Cal ID dashboard.
- **How to change** — select your preferred language from the **Language** dropdown list.

<Frame>
  ![Language dropdown](/images/settings/general-1.png)
</Frame>

### Timezone

- **What it does** — sets your main home timezone so all your meeting times are calculated correctly.
- **How to change** — select your local city or region from the **Timezone** dropdown menu.

<Frame>
  ![Timezone dropdown](/images/settings/general-2.png)
</Frame>

### Travel schedule

The travel schedule lets you plan a trip ahead of time to keep your existing schedule in a different timezone, so clients can't accidentally book you at midnight while you are traveling.

<Frame>
  ![Travel schedule section](/images/settings/general-3.png)
</Frame>

Set it up in a few steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a travel schedule">
    Click the **Add** button next to **Travel Schedule**.

    <Frame>
      ![Add travel schedule button](/images/settings/general-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your time range">
    Select the dates you will be traveling using the calendar picker.

    <Frame>
      ![Travel schedule date picker](/images/settings/general-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an open-ended trip (optional)">
    If you don't know when you are coming back, toggle **Schedule timezone without end date** to **ON**.

    <Frame>
      ![No end date toggle](/images/settings/general-6.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the destination timezone">
    Choose the timezone of the place you are visiting.

    <Frame>
      ![Travel timezone selector](/images/settings/general-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update">
    Click the blue **Update** button to save your changes and activate the travel schedule.

    <Frame>
      ![Updated travel schedule settings](/images/settings/general-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
