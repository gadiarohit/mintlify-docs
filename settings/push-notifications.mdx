Enable push notifications to get real-time browser alerts for booking activity — new bookings, cancellations, reschedules, and more — the moment they happen.

## Steps to enable push notifications

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Click your profile icon and select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Push Notifications page">
    Select **Push Notifications** from the settings menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow browser notifications">
    Click the **Allow Browser Notifications** button, then accept the permission prompt when your browser asks.

    <Frame>
      ![Allow browser notifications button](/images/settings/push-notifications-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You're done — Cal ID will now send booking alerts straight to your browser.
</Check>
