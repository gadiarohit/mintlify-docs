Cal ID gives you two availability profiles—your **personal availability** and your **team availability**—so you can manage shared team schedules alongside your own.

<Frame>
  ![Team availability schedules view](/images/availability/team-availability-1.png)
</Frame>

## Switch between views

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Availability">
    Click the **My Availability** tab to see your personal availability schedules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to Team Availability">
    Click the **Team Availability** tab to switch to team view. From here you can create, delete, and edit team availability schedules.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  You can create, delete, and edit team availability schedules only if you're an account **admin** or **owner**.
</Info>
