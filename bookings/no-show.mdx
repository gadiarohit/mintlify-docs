When an attendee doesn't appear, mark them as "No-Show" so your booking history reflects reality. In **Cal ID**, this keeps your records clean, distinguishes successful meetings from missed ones, and can trigger automated follow-up outreach.

## Mark a guest as no-show

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to past bookings">
    Go to the **Bookings** tab and select the **Past** sub-tab to see completed meetings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand booking details">
    Click the **Details** arrow on the specific booking you wish to manage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as no-show">
    Click the **Mark no-show** button located in the bottom-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unmark if necessary">
    If a mistake was made, click **Unmark no-show** in the same location to revert the status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![Mark no-show button on a past booking](/images/bookings/no-show-1.png)
</Frame>

<Info>
  No-show is only available for **past** bookings—meetings that have already taken place. Use the **Past** filter to isolate them.
</Info>

## Handling multiple attendees ('n' more)

For meetings with more than two attendees, Cal ID provides a streamlined way to manage group attendance.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the checklist">
    Click the **Mark as no show** button, which triggers a checklist popup for meetings with multiple participants.

    <Frame>
      ![No-show checklist popup for multiple attendees](/images/bookings/no-show-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage in bulk">
    Use the checklist to mark or unmark multiple guests as "No-Show" simultaneously.

    <Frame>
      ![Bulk marking attendees as no-show](/images/bookings/no-show-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

This ensures that even in large group sessions, you can accurately record which specific individuals were present.

## Attendance management overview

- **Filter view**: Use the **Past** filter to isolate meetings that have already occurred.
- **Attendee information**: View the attendee's name, timezone, and email address within the details section.

<Tip>
  **Professional follow-ups:** By marking a guest as a no-show, you keep your data clean for reporting. This status can also be used as a trigger for automated workflows, such as sending a personalized email to the guest asking them to reschedule.
</Tip>
