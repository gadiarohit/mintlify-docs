In [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home), the **Delete** tool allows you to permanently remove a meeting link that you no longer need. It is a simple way to keep your dashboard clean and ensure no one books a retired service.

## Deleting an event type

Deleting an event type removes it from your list and immediately disables the public booking link.

### Why delete an event type?

- **Clean your workspace**: Remove old or "test" events so you can find your active links quickly.
- **Stop new bookings**: If you are no longer offering a specific service, deleting the link prevents guests from scheduling time you don't want to give.
- **Control your availability**: It ensures that old links found in previous emails or social media posts are no longer active.

## How to do it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Event Types">
    **Login** to your [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home) account and navigate to the **Event Types** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the event">
    Find the event you want to remove from your list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the menu">
    Click the **three dots (···)** located on the right side of that event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Delete">
    Select **Delete** from the dropdown menu (it is highlighted in red).

    <Frame>
      ![Delete option in menu](/images/event-types/delete-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the choice in the popup box to permanently remove the event.

    <Frame>
      ![Delete confirmation popup](/images/event-types/delete-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  - **It is permanent**: Once you delete an event type, you cannot undo it or get the settings back.
  - **Existing bookings**: Deleting an event type will **not** delete meetings that are already on your calendar; it only stops **new** people from booking that specific type of meeting.
</Warning>
