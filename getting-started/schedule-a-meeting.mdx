Scheduling a meeting is a simple process of picking a time and securing your spot on the calendar.

<Note>
  If you are looking for your own link to send to a client, please refer to [How to Find and Share Your Cal ID URL](/getting-started/share-your-url).
</Note>

## Step 1: Open the booking link

- Open the Cal ID URL shared with you (e.g., `cal.id/username`).
- You will see all available **Event Types** (such as "Consultation," "Discovery Call," or "Technical Support").
- Click the **Schedule** button on the event you wish to book.

<Frame>
  ![Public booking page listing available event types](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-1.png)
</Frame>

## Step 2: Select your date and time

- **Select a Duration:** If the host allows multiple lengths for the meeting (e.g., 15m or 30m), select your preferred duration from the options on the left side of the page.
- **Pick a Date:** Select an available date from the calendar.
- **Pick a Time:** Choose a specific time slot that works for you.
- **Adjust Your Timezone:** While Cal ID **automatically** detects your location and sets the booking to your local timezone by default, you can manually change it using the timezone dropdown on the left to ensure perfect coordination.

<Frame>
  ![Calendar with date, time, duration, and timezone options](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-2.png)
</Frame>

## Step 3: Provide your details

- Answer the specific questions the host has included to help prepare for the meeting.
- **Payment (if applicable):** If the host has set a fee for the meeting, click **Pay to Book** to complete your payment securely via the integrated payment gateway.
- **Standard Booking:** For free meetings, simply click the **Confirm** button.

<Frame>
  ![Booking details form with attendee questions](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-3.png)
</Frame>

<Frame>
  ![Payment step for a paid booking](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-4.png)
</Frame>

## Step 4: Booking confirmation and approval

- **Instant Confirmation:** You will immediately see a **"Booking Confirmed"** page and also the Meeting URL (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.) will be sent directly to your Confirmation Email and added to your calendar invite.

<Frame>
  ![Booking Confirmed page](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-5.png)
</Frame>

- **Requires Confirmation:** If the host has enabled "Requires Confirmation," your booking is submitted as a request. You will receive a final confirmation or rejection email once the admin reviews it.

<Frame>
  ![Booking request submitted, awaiting host confirmation](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-6.png)
</Frame>

Meeting URL: Once confirmed, your Meeting URL (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.) will be sent directly to your Confirmation Email and added to your calendar invite.

## Step 5: Managing, cancelling, or rescheduling

Once your booking is finished, you can manage your appointment easily:

- **Two Ways to Manage:** You can reschedule or cancel your meeting directly from the **Booking Confirmation Page** (shown right after you book) or through the **Confirmation Email** you receive in your inbox.

<Frame>
  ![Reschedule and cancel options on the booking confirmation page](/images/getting-started/schedule-a-meeting-7.png)
</Frame>

- **How to Do It:** Simply click the "Reschedule" or "Cancel" link provided in those locations to choose a new time or notify the host of a cancellation.
- **Important Restriction:** To maintain a steady schedule, some hosts disable the cancellation and rescheduling features. If you do not see these options in your email or on the confirmation page, your booking is considered final, and you must contact the host directly for any changes.

That's it!
