The **Workflow Status** tool lets you see exactly what happens after a meeting is booked. It tracks every email or WhatsApp or SMS message sent by the system or by your custom workflows, so you always know if your attendee got the information.

## How to check the status

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Bookings">
    Click **Bookings** in the left menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Meeting">
    Look for the specific meeting in your list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Button">
    Click the **Workflow Status** button (it has a lightning bolt icon) on the right side of the booking.

    <Frame>
      ![Workflow Status button on a booking](/images/bookings/workflow-status-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the status words mean

When you open the status window, you will see a list of messages and their current stage:

- **Scheduled / Queued**: The message is waiting and will be sent at the right time (for example, "30 minutes before the meeting").

<Frame>
  ![Scheduled or Queued status](/images/bookings/workflow-status-2.png)
</Frame>

- **Delivered**: The message was successfully sent and reached the person's inbox or phone.

<Frame>
  ![Delivered status](/images/bookings/workflow-status-3.png)
</Frame>

- **Read**: The person has opened and looked at the message.

<Frame>
  ![Read status](/images/bookings/workflow-status-4.png)
</Frame>

- **Bounced**: The message could not be delivered. This usually happens if the email address is wrong or the inbox is full.

<Frame>
  ![Bounced status](/images/bookings/workflow-status-5.png)
</Frame>

- **Failed**: There was an error, and the message could not be sent.

<Frame>
  ![Failed status](/images/bookings/workflow-status-6.png)
</Frame>

- **Cancelled**: If you cancel the meeting, the system automatically stops all future messages and marks them as "Cancelled".

<Frame>
  ![Cancelled status](/images/bookings/workflow-status-7.png)
</Frame>

## Why this is helpful

- **No More Guessing**: If a client says they didn't get the link, you can check here to see if it **Bounced** or was **Delivered**.
- **Check Attendance**: If the status shows **Read**, you know the attendee is aware of the meeting.
- **Automatic Cleanup**: You don't have to worry about reminders going out for a cancelled meeting; the system handles the **Cancellation** for you.
