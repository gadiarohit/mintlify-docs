---
title: "How to Find and Share Your Cal ID URL"
description: "Find your personal Cal ID booking link and share it from the dashboard, profile settings, or event types."
icon: "link"
---

Your Cal ID URL is your personal booking link. It is the address you share with others so they can view your availability and schedule meetings with you.

## Method 1: Using the Home Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your account">
    **Log in** to your Cal ID account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the checklist">
    On the **Home** tab, find the **Let's get you started!** checklist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your Cal ID">
    Click the button labeled **Share your Cal ID**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your link">
    A pop-up window will appear showing your link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy or share">
    Click **Copy** to save the link or choose a social platform (like LinkedIn, WhatsApp, or Twitter) to share it directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Method 2: Using Profile Settings (View or Change Username)

If you want to see your link or customize it to something more professional, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings** in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Profile">
    Select the **Profile** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Username field">
    Look for the **Username** field. This name is what appears at the end of your link (e.g., `cal.id/yourname`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="To Copy">
    Simply highlight and copy the text in this field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To Change">
    You can edit this field to update your username.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Changing your username will change your booking URL. Make sure to update any links you have already shared!
</Note>

<Frame>
  ![Username field in Profile settings](/images/getting-started/share-your-url-3.png)

## Method 3: Using the Event Types Page

You can also find and copy your main Cal ID URL directly from the dashboard where you manage your event types.

- Navigate to **Event Types** in the left-hand sidebar.
- At the top of the page, next to the search bar, you will see your general booking link (e.g., `cal.id/your-username`).
- Click the **copy icon** (two overlapping squares) next to the URL to save it to your clipboard.

<Frame>
  ![Booking link and copy icon on the Event Types page](/images/getting-started/share-your-url-4.png)
</Frame>

- Click the **external link icon** (arrow pointing out of a square) to open your public booking page in a new tab.

## Method 4: Using Specific Event Types

If you want to share a link for a specific meeting type (like an "Interview" or "Introductory Call") rather than your general profile, follow these steps:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Event Types">
    Navigate to the **Event Types** tab in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the event">
    Locate the specific event you wish to share from your list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="To Copy quickly">
    Click the **copy icon** (two overlapping squares) located next to the event URL in the event card.

    <Frame>
      ![Copy icon next to an event URL on the event card](/images/getting-started/share-your-url-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="To Preview or Copy from the editor">
    Click on the **Event Name** to open the editor. You will find the full URL under the **URL** field within the **Event Setup** tab.

    <Frame>
      ![URL field in the Event Setup tab of the event editor](/images/getting-started/share-your-url-6.png)
    </Frame>

    Click the **copy icon** there or click the **external link icon** to view the live booking page before sharing.

    <Frame>
      ![Live booking page preview for a specific event type](/images/getting-started/share-your-url-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
