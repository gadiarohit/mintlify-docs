Give every event type its own look. In [**Cal ID**](https://cal.id/home), the **Change icon** feature lets you pick a unique symbol and accent color for each event, so your dashboard and public booking page stay organized and on-brand.

## Adding custom icons to events

An icon is the small symbol that represents your meeting. Adding a custom icon helps you stay organized and makes your page look professional.

### Why use custom icons?

- **Visual organization**: Easily tell the difference between a "Group Session" and a "One-on-One" call by using different symbols.
- **Brand matching**: Use your brand's specific colors to make your booking page feel like a part of your business.
- **Guest clarity**: Icons like a 🎓 for "Career Counselling" or a 🛠️ for a "Workshop" help guests understand what the meeting is about at a glance.

## How to do it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Change icon">
    Login to your **Cal ID** account and go to the **Event Types** tab. Find the event you want to change, click the **three dots (···)**, and select **Change icon**.

    <Frame>
      ![Change icon option](/images/event-types/custom-icons-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a symbol">
    A **Choose Icon** box will pop up on your screen.

    - **Search**: Use the search bar to find a specific icon (like "Video" or "Phone").
    - **Categories**: Browse icons by category, such as Business, Communication, or Education.

    <Frame>
      ![Choose Icon box](/images/event-types/custom-icons-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a color">
    - Click on the **Icon Color** box at the bottom.
    - Enter a specific color code (HEX) or choose a color that matches your brand.

    <Frame>
      ![Icon color picker](/images/event-types/custom-icons-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Click **Apply** to save your new look.

    <Frame>
      ![Applied custom icon](/images/event-types/custom-icons-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For faster visual organization, use color as a system: keep the same color for all "Discovery" meetings and a different color for all "Paid" meetings. A consistent palette lets you scan your schedule at a glance.
</Tip>
