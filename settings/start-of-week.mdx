The **Start of week** setting lets you choose which day of the week appears first on your calendar. Changing it reorganizes the layout of your **public booking page** to match your preferred work schedule.

## Why change the start of the week?

The day you select becomes the "anchor" for your calendar grid.

- **Standard business view** — many professionals prefer a **Monday** start so the work week (Monday–Friday) is grouped together.
- **Traditional view** — selecting **Sunday** places the weekend at the start of the calendar grid.
- **Visual only** — this change only moves the columns; it does **not** change your available hours or existing meetings.

## Update your calendar layout

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    Locate the sidebar on the left side of your dashboard and click **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the General tab">
    Inside the settings menu, click the **General** sub-tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Start of week">
    Scroll down the page until you see the dropdown menu labeled **Start of week**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your day">
    Click the menu and select your preferred day (for example, **Monday**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the change">
    Click the blue **Update** button at the bottom of the screen to save your new layout.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What your guests will see

Once you click **Update**, your public booking page immediately reflects the new layout.

- **New column order** — if you selected **Monday**, the first column on the far left is **MON**, followed by **TUE**, and ending with **SUN** on the far right.
- **Correct dates** — all your dates automatically shift to stay under the correct day (e.g., if February 5th is a Thursday, it stays under the **THU** column regardless of which day the week starts).
- **Easy navigation** — this makes it much easier for clients to view your availability based on the work week they are used to.

<Tip>
  Most international businesses use a **Monday** start to keep the work week clear for clients. If you primarily work with clients in North America, a **Sunday** start is often the most familiar choice.
</Tip>
