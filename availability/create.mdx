Your **Availability Schedule** is the foundation of your calendar. By defining your "Active Hours," you stay in total control of your time—meetings only happen when you're available, while your personal time stays protected for family, breaks, and rest.

You aren't limited to a single set of hours. You can use one availability schedule across all event types, share a schedule between a few of them, or give each event type its own schedule to keep things organized.

## Set up your availability

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Availability">
    On your main dashboard, locate the left-hand menu and click on **Availability**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or edit a schedule">
    You can create **multiple schedules** to fit different needs.

    - **Create:** Click the **\+ New** button to create a specific schedule.
    - **Edit:** To change an existing one, click on the name of the schedule you want to update.

    <Frame>
      ![Availability schedule list with the New button](/images/availability/create-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your timezone">
    Before setting your hours, verify the **Timezone** at the top of the page. This ensures that when a client books a time, it aligns perfectly with your local clock.

    <Frame>
      ![Timezone selector at the top of the schedule](/images/availability/create-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your weekly hours">
    This is where you define your availability:

    - **Toggle days:** Use the switches next to each day to set them to **ON** or **OFF**.
    - **Set time slots:** Enter your start and end times (e.g., 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
    - **Add a break (split-shift):** To block out a lunch break or school run, click the **"\+" icon** to add a second time slot on the same day.
    - **Example:** Set **9:00 AM – 12:00 PM** and **1:00 PM – 5:00 PM**. The system will automatically keep you unbookable during that 12:00–1:00 hour.

    <Frame>
      ![Weekly hours with day toggles and time slots](/images/availability/create-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Once you have organized your hours, click the **Save** button. Your schedule is now ready to be linked to your event types.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  Your schedule is saved and ready to link to any event type.
</Check>
