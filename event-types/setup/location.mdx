Set where each meeting happens—a video call, a physical office, or a phone call—and even let guests choose their preferred option at booking time. In **Cal ID**, the location is defined per event type, so different meetings can meet in different places.

## Supported location types

Cal ID offers a wide variety of ways to connect. When setting up your event, you can choose from any of the following:

| Location type | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **In Person** | Ideal for physical venues. Accepts a text input where you type a specific address or office name. |
| **Link** | Use a custom or permanent meeting link (like a static personal Zoom room or a proprietary URL). |
| **Attendee Phone Number** | Cal ID prompts the guest to provide their phone number during booking so you can call them. |
| **Organizer Phone Number** | You provide your own phone number as the designated meeting spot for the guest to call. |
| **Jitsi Video Global** | Use Jitsi's native global video conferencing as your booking location. |
| **Third-Party Web Conference** | Select from integrated apps like **Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams**, and more. |

<Note>
  Third-party web conferencing apps must be integrated from the [**Cal ID App Store**](/apps/install-app) before they appear as a location option.
</Note>

## How to set or change the location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Event Types">
    Navigate to the **Event Types** in your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an event">
    Click on the event you want to customize.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Location section">
    Scroll down to the **Location** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a location">
    Click the **Add Location** button or use the dropdown menu to see your options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in details">
    Select your desired location and fill in any required details (like an address or phone number).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update** or **Save** at the top of the page.

    <Frame>
      ![Location section in event settings](/images/event-types/location-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Advanced features

### Multiple locations (attendee choice)

You can offer more than one way to meet for a single event. For example, you can let a guest choose between a "Zoom Call" and an "In-person Meeting." Simply add multiple location options in the event settings.

<Frame>
  ![Multiple location options](/images/event-types/location-4.png)
</Frame>

When the guest selects their time slot, they will see an **additional required question** on the booking form. They must pick their preferred location from your list before they can confirm the meeting.

<Frame>
  ![Location choice on the booking form](/images/event-types/location-5.png)
</Frame>

### Privacy option: show after booking

For sensitive locations (like a private office or home address), you can choose to hide the details from the public booking page. The full location will only be revealed to the guest in the **Confirmation Email** after they have successfully booked.
