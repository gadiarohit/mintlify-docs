The **Appearance** settings let you personalize how Cal ID looks—both the private dashboard you work in and the public booking page your guests see. Set a theme for each, then choose how your available times are laid out.

## Complete setup steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Sign in to your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Settings">
    Click the **Settings** icon in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Appearance tab">
    Open the **Appearance** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize your appearance">
    Set your themes and booking layout using the sections below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click the final **Update** button at the very bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each section on this page has its own **Update** button. Save the section you just changed before moving on.
</Note>

## Setting your dashboard theme

This controls the appearance of your logged-in dashboard.

<Note>
  The dashboard theme is for your eyes only—it does not affect what your customers see.
</Note>

| Theme | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **System Default** | Automatically matches your computer or browser's light/dark mode settings. |
| **Light** | A clean, bright interface with a white background. |
| **Dark** | A high-contrast dark interface, which can be easier on the eyes in low-light environments. |

## Setting your public booking page theme

This determines the theme your guests see when they visit your link to book a meeting. Just like the dashboard, you can choose between **System default**, **Light**, or **Dark**.

<Tip>

## Choosing and customizing your booking layout

The **Booking layout** determines how your available time slots are presented to bookers. You decide which views are available and which one they see first.

### The three layout options

| Layout | What guests see | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Month view** | A standard grid showing the entire month. | Booking far in advance and seeing high-level availability. |
| **Weekly view** | A side-by-side comparison of days within a specific week. | Finding an immediate opening within the next few days. |
| **Column view** | A sleek, vertical list of dates followed by their specific time slots. | The most mobile-friendly view, great for quick selection. |

### Enabling multiple views

By checking the boxes for **Month**, **Weekly**, and **Column**, you allow your bookers to switch between these layouts manually using buttons on your public page.

<Frame>
  ![Enable multiple layout views](/images/appearance/appearance-9.png)
</Frame>

If you only want your users to see one specific style, uncheck the other two.

<Frame>
  ![Single layout view enabled](/images/appearance/appearance-10.png)
</Frame>

### Setting the default view

Under the **Default view** header, you will see buttons for Month, Weekly, and Column. Select the one you want to load automatically when someone clicks your booking link.

<Frame>
  ![Set default booking view](/images/appearance/appearance-11.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  To provide the most flexibility, check all three layout boxes (**Month**, **Weekly**, and **Column**). This allows your bookers to switch to the view they find most comfortable while navigating your schedule.
</Tip>
