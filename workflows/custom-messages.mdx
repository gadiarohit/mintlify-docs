Choosing a custom template in Cal ID gives you full creative control over how your reminders and notifications look. Here is a clear breakdown of how you can personalize your workflows.

The Cal ID Workflow system is designed for flexibility. While the **Default Template** is great for a quick setup, switching to a **Custom Template** unlocks several advanced personalization options:

## Enable customization

To start personalizing, first go to the **Message template** field and select **Custom** from the dropdown menu. This action unlocks the ability to edit the Sender Name, Subject, and Email Body.

## Personalize the sender identity

When you select a custom template, you aren't stuck with a generic "Cal ID" name.

- **Sender Name:** You can change this to your own name, your company's name, or even the name of a specific department to build trust with your attendees.

<Frame>
  ![Editing the sender name](/images/workflows/custom-messages-2.png)
</Frame>

## Craft a dynamic subject line

The subject line is the first thing your attendees see. With a custom template:

- **Custom Subject:** You can write a catchy, relevant subject line from scratch.
- **Dynamic Variables:** Don't just say "Meeting Reminder." Use the **Add variable** dropdown to include the `{EVENT_NAME}` or `{ATTENDEE}` name directly in the subject to make it stand out in a crowded inbox.

  ![Adding a dynamic variable to the subject line](/images/workflows/custom-messages-3.png)

## Use the power of variables

To ensure every email feels personal and contains accurate data, always use the **Add variable** dropdown menu located in the Subject and Email Body sections.

**Commonly used variables include:**

- `{EVENT_NAME}`: The title of the meeting.
- `{EVENT_DATE}`: Automatically formats the date of the session.
- `{MEETING_URL}`: Drops in the specific link for your video call (Zoom, Google Meet, etc.).
- `{LOCATION}`: Provides the physical address or meeting details
- `{ATTENDEE}`: Full Name of the attendee
- `{ATTENDEE_FIRST_NAME}`: First Name of the attendee
- `{ATTENDEE_LAST_NAME}`: Last Name of the attendee
- `{CANCEL_URL}`: URL for cancelling the meeting
- `{ORGANIZER_NAME}`: Name of the Organizer
- `{RESCHEDULE_URL}`: URL for rescheduling the meeting
- `{ATTENDEE_TIMEZONE}`: Timezone of the attendee

<Note>
  You can't customize the template of SMS workflow.
</Note>

If you want to customize the template for WhatsApp, then you have to [Integrate WhatsApp Business App](/workflows/whatsapp-business).
