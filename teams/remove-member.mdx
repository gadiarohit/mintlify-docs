Take a member off a team when they no longer need access. Removing someone revokes their access to the team's shared events and frees up their assignments for reassignment.

<Info>
  Only **Owners** or **Admins** have permission to remove members from a team.
</Info>

## Remove a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Cal ID">
    Access your account dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Teams">
    Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access the team menu">
    Locate the team, click the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Edit team**.

    <Frame>
      ![Edit team option](/images/teams/remove-member-1.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Members tab">
    Click on the **Members** tab at the top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate removal">
    Locate the member you wish to remove, click the **three horizontal dots** next to their name, and select **Remove**.

    <Frame>
      ![Remove option next to a member](/images/teams/remove-member-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the action">
    Follow any on-screen prompts to confirm the removal.

    <Frame>
      ![Confirming member removal](/images/teams/remove-member-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Removing a member revokes their access to the team's shared event types and booking links. Any bookings assigned to them will need to be reassigned, so review their upcoming meetings before you remove them.
</Warning>
