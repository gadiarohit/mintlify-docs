## How to create a new WhatsApp Business Account?

To Create a New WhatsApp Business Account: Select "**Create a business portfolio**" and "Create a **WhatsApp Business account**" in the respective field.

**Complete the Setup** - Fill all required fields and save

**Once Created**: Your WhatsApp Business account will be ready. You can now connect it with Cal ID, refer [Connect with WhatsApp Business App](/workflows/connect-whatsapp-app).

<Note>
  You must verify your Tax ID on Meta to trigger the templates.
</Note>
