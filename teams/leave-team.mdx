This document explains how to remove yourself from a team you are currently a member of.

## Steps to leave a team

1. **Log in to Cal ID**: Access your account dashboard.
2. **Go to Teams**: Click on the **Teams** icon in the left-hand sidebar.
3. **Initiate Leave**: Locate the team you wish to leave, click the **three horizontal dots**, and select **Leave team**.

<Frame>
  ![Leave team option](/images/teams/leave-team-1.png)
</Frame>

4. **Confirm Action**: A dialog box will appear asking to confirm your decision. Click the red **Yes, leave team** button.

<Frame>
  ![Confirming leaving the team](/images/teams/leave-team-2.png)
</Frame>

<Note>
  Once you leave a team, you will no longer have access to the team's shared event types or booking links.
</Note>
